# QQCI Semantic Dictionary — Development & Editing Protocols

This skill enforces all 10 core development protocols, schema standards, and editing guardrails for the 44-word QQCI Semantic Dictionary in `Reality Class'ification project/`.

---

## 1. Zero-Tolerance Language Standards: Generic Process English
Language must be **pure, generic, physical, and informational process English**:
* **NO Systems / Tech Jargon**: Banned terms include `state execution`, `telemetry`, `autopoiesis`, `gradients`, `state-matrix`, `subsystems`, `processes`, `entropy`, `taxonomic`, `O(1)`, `ontological`.
* **NO Biological / Anthropomorphic Parochialism**: Banned narrow biological clichés include `mouth`, `stomach`, `eating`, `food`, `feeding`, `starving`, `breathing`, `cellular metabolism`, `deathbed`, `grave`.
* **The "Universal Invariant" Test**: The wording must describe a natural physical or informational process that is equally true for **a fire, an engine, a star, a software program, a person, a town, an idea, or a wave**.
* Write with plain, simple, grounded words that a 12-year-old or someone 500 years ago would immediately understand.

## 2. The Universal Force Vector Triad ($\text{Action}_i \xrightarrow{\text{Strain}_i} \text{Ideal}_i$)
Every word is defined by a strict 3-way pairwise vector equation:

$$\mathbf{Word\ Vector} = \Big[\ \mathbf{Current}\ (\text{Action}),\ \ \mathbf{Strain}\ (\text{Distance}),\ \ \mathbf{Ideal}\ (\text{Effect})\ \Big]$$

Every entry **MUST** adhere to strict 1-to-1 pairwise lockstep across its 3 steps:
1. **`Current` (Action)**: The 3 progressive physical actions occurring right now (`Action_1, Action_2, Action_3`).
2. **`Strain` (Distance $\sigma_i = \|\text{Ideal}_i - \text{Action}_i\|$ )**: The exact 3-step impedance, tension, or disordered state that each action step overcomes (`Strain_1, Strain_2, Strain_3`).
3. **`Ideal` (Effect)**: The 3 resulting stable equilibrium states achieved once each action step succeeds (`Ideal_1, Ideal_2, Ideal_3`).

### The Pairwise Invariant (The `store` Benchmark):
$$\begin{array}{rcccl}
\text{Step 1:} & \mathbf{Action}_1\ (\text{put in}) & \xrightarrow[\mathbf{Strain}_1\ (\text{exposed})]{\quad} & \mathbf{Effect}_1\ (\text{safe}) \\
\text{Step 2:} & \mathbf{Action}_2\ (\text{keep organized}) & \xrightarrow[\mathbf{Strain}_2\ (\text{cluttered})]{\quad} & \mathbf{Effect}_2\ (\text{ordered}) \\
\text{Step 3:} & \mathbf{Action}_3\ (\text{take out}) & \xrightarrow[\mathbf{Strain}_3\ (\text{inaccessible})]{\quad} & \mathbf{Effect}_3\ (\text{ready to use})
\end{array}$$

* **`action`**: `"put in, keep organized, take out"`
* **`strain`**: `"exposed, cluttered, inaccessible"`
* **`effect`**: `"safe, ordered, ready to use"`
* **`definitive_meaning`**: Plain English sentence synthesizing the 3-step action and 3-step effect (*"to keep things in an organized place for later retrieval"*).

## 3. Conjugate Dual Invariants (Opposing Root Pairs)
For inverse root pairs (e.g. `live`/`die`, `build`/`break`, `give`/`take`, `go`/`come`):
* The starting state of one vector is the active state of its opposite.
* The target ideal of one vector is the resolved cessation/inverse of the other.
* **Their Strains are each other's opposing forces**:
  * The Strain resisting **`live`** is the pull of **`die`** (`drained, jammed, stopped`).
  * The Strain resisting **`die`** is the persistence of **`live`** (`holding on, resisting, lingering`).

## 4. The Plane-Collapse Ban (Strictly Plane-Agnostic Root Definitions)
Root genotypes exist in the invariant root vector space and must be **strictly plane-agnostic**:
* **NEVER** use plane-specific adjectives in the root `definitive_meaning`, `action`, `strain`, or `ideal` (e.g. Banned in root definitions: `physical`, `mental`, `emotional`, `biological`, `historical`, `social`, `logical`).
* **Why it Breaks**: Saying *"shelter is a physical boundary..."* forcibly locks a universal 7-plane concept into **Plane Q3 (Physical)**, breaking its manifestations in $Q_1$ (Identity), $Q_2$ (Possibility), $Q_4$ (Meaning), $Q_5$ (Logic), $Q_6$ (History), and $Q_7$ (Emotion).
* The root force equation applies universally across all 7 dimensions; specific plane expressions appear **only** inside that plane's row ($Q_1 \dots Q_7$) in the projection table.

## 5. Orthogonal Vocabulary Dimension & Divergence
The 44 root genotypes form the **orthogonal semantic coordinate basis of reality**:
* **Irreducible Primitives**: Each root genotype represents a pure, non-overlapping primitive of reality (containment, motion, cessation, generation, division, transformation).
* **Divergence into Children**: Whenever a concept is discovered to be a composite or downstream specialization anchored to specific planes or mechanisms (e.g. `shop` = `store` anchored to $Q_2/Q_3$; `library` = `store` anchored to $Q_4/Q_3$), it is pushed out from the root basis into a **divergent child phenotype**.
* **Purity of the Root Basis**: Root words must remain totally unanchored to any specific plane, medium, or scale so they can generate any possible child phenotype without contradiction.

## 6. Words as Purpose-Built Problem-Solving Tools (Residue of Intent)
A word is not an arbitrary linguistic label; it is a **purpose-built tool invented by an agent to solve a specific tension, obstacle, or problem in reality**:
* **The Origin of Primitives**: When an agent encounters an obstacle or deficit (e.g. a boundary blocking traversal $\to$ `open`; incoming hostile force $\to$ `shelter`; scarcity across time $\to$ `store`), they direct an **Action** vector against that **Strain** to achieve a stable **Effect**.
* **The Residue of Intent**: A word is the crystallized operational formula invented to repeat, coordinate, and navigate that state transition.
* **The AE-C Universal Equation**:
  $$\mathbf{Word} = \big(\ \mathbf{Action} \;\xrightarrow{\mathbf{Strain}}\; \mathbf{Effect}\ \big)\ \Big\vert_{\ \mathbf{Context}}$$
  Where **Context** is the dimensional Q-plane ($Q_1 \dots Q_7$) + the relational physical anchor (boundary, floor, observer, capacity).

## 7. Strict Plane Separation (Zero Cross-Plane Bleed)
Each plane describes strictly its own dimensional slice:
* **$Q_1$ (Who / Identity)**: Agency, Observer, Selfhood (*Only the agent/observer*).
* **$Q_2$ (What / Possible)**: Potential, Options, Raw Material, Signatures (*Only the uncommitted capacity/traits*).
* **$Q_3$ (Where / Physical)**: Bounded Space, Distance, Physical Container, Interfaces (*Only physical mass/boundary, e.g. "roofs, walls, and hulls shielding mass from the environment"*).
* **$Q_4$ (Why / Meaning)**: **The ONLY plane where "Why", "Reasons", and "Purpose" ("so that...", "in order to...", "to prevent...") are permitted**.
* **$Q_5$ (How / Logical)**: Mechanism, Process, Schema, Rules, Taxonomy (*Only how it operates*).
* **$Q_6$ (Cause / Historical)**: Origin, Past Inflow, Memory Templates, Prior Precedent (*Only the timeline origin/intake*).
* **$Q_7$ (Effect / Emotive)**: Result, Outflow, State Resolution, Active Utility (*Only the consequence/payoff*).

> **Rule**: **NEVER put "so that...", "in order to...", "to prevent...", or purpose justifications in planes $Q_1, Q_2, Q_3, Q_5, Q_6, Q_7$**. All driving reasons and telos belong strictly in $Q_4$.

## 8. Genotype vs. Phenotype Architecture (Dual Anchors)

### A. Root Concepts (Genotypes)
* Universal primitives (`is_fundamental: true`, `anchor_type: "root_concept"`, `concept_type: "genotype"`, `parent: null`).
* Define the base force vector: $\mathbf{Word}(\mathbf{Action}[\text{Current}],\ \mathbf{Effect}[\text{Ideal}])$.
* Manifest across all 7 planes of reality.
* *Example*: `store` is the root genotype.

### B. Child Words (Phenotypes)
* Specializations born from a parent genotype (`concept_type: "child"`, `is_fundamental: false`).
* Defined by **Dual Anchors**:
  1. **`body_anchor`**: Where it manifests physically/spatially (typically $Q_3$ Physical / Location).
  2. **`purpose_anchor`**: The driving plane that gives it its specific function and reason to exist ($Q_1 \dots Q_7$).
* *Examples*:
  * **`shop`**: Child of `store` | Body: $Q_3$ (Physical storefront) | Purpose: $Q_2$ (Commercial exchange & sale).
  * **`library`**: Child of `store` | Body: $Q_3$ (Physical hall/stacks) | Purpose: $Q_4$ (Preserving & sharing knowledge).
  * **`database`**: Child of `store` | Body: $Q_3$ (Digital disk/server) | Purpose: $Q_5$ (Logical query & fast indexing).

## 10. Plane Projections: Interrogative Answer & Structural Isomorphism
Every plane ($Q_1 \dots Q_7$) in an entry maps the word across two distinct theoretical components:

1. **`answer` (The Interrogative Answer)**:
   The direct, plain-English answer to the plane's interrogative question (*Who/What/Where/Why/How/Cause/Effect*) for that word.
   * **$Q_1$ (Who?)**: *Who is the store?* $\to$ `"the keeper or holder of the items"`
   * **$Q_2$ (What?)**: *What is in the store?* $\to$ `"the unspent capacity and uncommitted resources"`
   * **$Q_3$ (Where?)**: *Where is the store located?* $\to$ `"the physical or digital place where items sit"`
   * **$Q_4$ (Why?)**: *Why do we store?* $\to$ `"preserving purpose and foresight for the future"`
   * **$Q_5$ (How?)**: *How does storing operate?* $\to$ `"arranging and indexing for identification and retrieval"`
   * **$Q_6$ (Cause?)**: *What caused the store?* $\to$ `"past intake and surplus that created the need to store"`
   * **$Q_7$ (Effect?)**: *What is the outcome of storing?* $\to$ `"readiness and peace of mind knowing supplies exist"`

2. **`word_in_context_of_plane` (The Structural Isomorphism)**:
   The exact mathematical isomorphism mapping the root invariant force equation ($\mathbf{Action}[\text{Current}] \xrightarrow[\mathbf{Strain}]{\quad} \mathbf{Effect}[\text{Ideal}]$) into that plane's reality space:
   `"[dimension] as [anchor] — [plain English operational isomorphism]"`
   * *Example*: `"identity as a store — holding memories, habits, and will in reserve so the self remains safe, ordered, and ready to act"`

## 12. The 6 Invariant Checkpoints for Every Entry

Before adding or updating any word, verify against these 6 rules:

1. **Strict 1-to-1 Triad Lockstep**:
   $$\begin{array}{rcccl}
   \text{Step 1 (Inception/Intake):} & \mathbf{Action}_1 & \xrightarrow{\mathbf{Strain}_1} & \mathbf{Effect}_1 \\
   \text{Step 2 (Operation/Transit):} & \mathbf{Action}_2 & \xrightarrow{\mathbf{Strain}_2} & \mathbf{Effect}_2 \\
   \text{Step 3 (Completion/State):} & \mathbf{Action}_3 & \xrightarrow{\mathbf{Strain}_3} & \mathbf{Effect}_3
   \end{array}$$
2. **The Plane-Collapse Ban**:
   * Root `definitive_meaning` and triad must NOT contain plane-collapsing words (`physical`, `mental`, `emotional`, `biological`, `social`, `logical`, `brain`, `body`).
3. **Raw Information / Substrate Neutrality**:
   * Applies universally whether to an engine, a star, a software algorithm, a living organism, or a human community.
   * Zero carbon-based biological clichés (`food`, `eating`, `mouth`, `starving`) and zero tech jargon (`state execution`, `telemetry`, `subsystems`).
4. **Conjugate Polar Duality**:
   * If the word has an opposite, verify that its **Strain** is the active resistance of its conjugate ($\text{Strain}(\mathbf{live}) = \text{pull of } \mathbf{die}$; $\text{Strain}(\mathbf{die}) = \text{pull of } \mathbf{live}$).
5. **Strict Plane Isolation (Telos Exclusively in Q4)**:
   * Purpose clauses (*"so that..."*, *"in order to..."*) belong **strictly in Q4**. Never put them in Q1, Q2, Q3, Q5, Q6, or Q7.
6. **Goldilocks Word Count**:
   * `answer`: 4 to 8 words.
   * `word_in_context_of_plane`: 15 to 25 words formatted strictly as `"[dimension] as [word] — [isomorphism]"`.

## 13. Surgical Editing & Synchronization Workflow
Whenever any dictionary entry is added or edited:
1. Edit **`Reality Class'ification project/qqci_dictionary.json`**.
2. Run the automated protocol validator:
   ```powershell
   python "Reality Class'ification project/_AI files and chat logs/validate_dictionary.py"
   ```
3. Rebuild the JS distribution and sync v2 copies:
   ```powershell
   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "Reality Class'ification project/build_dict_js.ps1"
   Copy-Item "Reality Class'ification project/qqci_dictionary.json" "Reality Class'ification project/qqci_dictionary_v2.json"
   Copy-Item "Reality Class'ification project/qqci_dictionary.js" "Reality Class'ification project/qqci_dictionary_v2.js"
   ```
4. Verify the visualizer in the browser (open `qqci_dictionary_viewer.html` or `qqci_dictionary_viewer_v2.html`).

* **No Wholesale Overwrites**: When fixing or updating entries, make surgical edits via `replace_file_content` targeting the specific line or field. Do not run destructive scripts that overwrite the entire JSON file from scratch.
* **Updating Entries**: Entries can and should be updated to fix errors, improve clarity, or meet protocol rules. Keep updates strictly aligned with the 10 protocols above.
* **Build Synchronization**: After editing `qqci_dictionary.json`, immediately run `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "Reality Class'ification project/build_dict_js.ps1"` to compile `qqci_dictionary.js`.
* **Validation**: Run `python "Reality Class'ification project/_AI files and chat logs/validate_dictionary.py"` to ensure 100% compliance across all 44 entries.
