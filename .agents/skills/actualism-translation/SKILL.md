# Action-Effect over Context (AE-C) & Plane–State Translation Method

A comprehensive Actualism hermeneutic integrating micro-scale word/identifier root addition, meso-scale passage plane translation, and macro-scale myth/legend plane–state causal deconstruction.

---

## Three Distinct Formats — Choose Before Starting

### 1. Passage & Decree Format (Format A)
* **Scope:** Sentences, verses, prophetic decrees, specific dialogues, or blocks of philosophical/scriptural text.
* **Structure:** Original Text $\to$ Standard Translation $\to$ Contextual Vector Duality (2–3 key terms) $\to$ $Q_1$–$Q_7$ Plane Translations.
* **Rule:** Do not include a root analysis section for passages. Expanding every word in a passage expands indefinitely and obscures the function. The vector duality and the $Q_1$–$Q_7$ plane renderings carry the load.

### 2. Identifier & Name Format (Format B)
* **Scope:** Proper nouns, names of gods/heroes, titles, archetypes, relics, weapons, or institutional labels.
* **Structure:** Root Analysis (additive sum of all dictionary definitions) $\to$ Machine Code Summary $\to$ Contextual Vector Duality $\to$ $Q_1$–$Q_7$ Plane Translations applied to a key passage or functional context.

### 3. Myth, Legend & Narrative Arc Format (Format C)
* **Scope:** Continuous stories, parables, folklore, mythic cycles, allegories, and scripture chapters.
* **Structure:** Granular Moving Frame Deconstruction (Active Planes $\to$ Belief States $\to$ Social Reception $\to$ Order/Chaos Classification $\to$ State Transitions $\to$ Truth Functions $\to$ Abstracted Invariant Retelling $\to$ Validation Check) $\to$ Macro Framework Synthesis.

## Core Principles

1. **Language as Event Physics:** A word is not a noun floating in a vacuum. It is a pointer to a physical or conceptual *event* taking place in reality. Its meaning is not its label — it is the action-effect geometry it describes, within the specific contextual frame in which it operates.
2. **Additive Arithmetic, Not Subjective Interpretation:** Every translation is the orthodox, dictionary-standard translation of each root word added together in sequence. No meaning is invented or retrofitted. The compound of all root definitions IS the meaning.
3. **Myth and Legend as Action Engines:** Greek myth, biblical allegory, folklore, and ancient philosophy encode action, constraint, and systemic dynamics, not static description. Meaning is generated by how personal and collective belief states move across planes of reality under constraint over time.

## The Four Language Axioms

* **Axiom I:** Words describe actions and events, not static objects.
* **Axiom II:** Language operates by tracking actions within a contextual frame. The frame is not optional — it grants the word its meaning.
* **Axiom III:** Words are rooted ultimately in physical reality, not arbitrary agreement. Arbitrary agreement can drift. Physical constraint cannot.
* **Axiom IV:** Literacy is world-competence, not symbol decoding.

## Contextual Vector Duality ($\upsilon$-Axis)

Every word, name, identifier, or character encodes a *function*, not a fixed moral state. The same mechanism operates in two directions:

* **Order Vector ($+\upsilon$):** The function operating in alignment with the Greater Good / systemic coherence — what the entity or process is *for* when correctly calibrated.
* **Chaos Vector ($-\upsilon$):** The same function inverted — extraction, obstruction, deception, or misalignment. Same mechanism, opposite direction.

The name or archetype describes the functional capacity; it does not describe the temporary calibration of the bearer.

Load-bearing functional terms take on the plane’s register, while structural connective words remain in natural flowing prose.

## The 8-Step Plane–State Myth & Narrative Deconstruction Protocol

When analyzing myths, legends, scriptures, parables, or continuous narrative arcs (Format C), execute the following 8 steps for each frame:

### Step 1: Identify the Active Planes
For each scene, character, element, or moving frame, determine which of the seven planes are actively engaged:
* **Physical ($Q_3$):** Material actions, bodies, landscapes, kinetic weapons, biological constraints.
* **Lyrical ($Q_4$):** Spoken decrees, songs, curses, poetic prophecies, symbolic motifs.
* **Emotional ($Q_7$):** Fear, grief, lust, envy, pride, relational attachment/aversion.
* **Historical ($Q_6$):** Lineages, ancestral memory, inherited debts, temporal precedents.
* **Possible ($Q_2$):** Prophecies of potential futures, unchosen paths, branching choices.
* **Logical ($Q_5$):** Covenants, transactional contracts, structural laws, conditional logic.
* **Conscious / Metaphysical ($Q_1$):** Self-awareness, moral reflection, alignment with prime will.
*(Note: Not all planes must be active in every scene. The total absence of a plane is an active diagnostic signal.)*

### Step 2: Assign Personal Belief States per Active Plane
For each active agent in the frame, assign their operative belief state (`=`, `+?`, `<`, `~?`, `>`, `-?`) per plane. This isolates the agent's exact operational posture rather than relying on vague moral labels.

### Step 3: Determine Social and Systemic Reception
Evaluate how the agent’s internal belief state is received by other actors, hierarchies, or the surrounding system:
* Is **Truth `(=)`** treated as stabilizing or as an existential threat to the hierarchy?
* Is **Inquiry `(+?)`** rewarded or punished as heresy/treason?
* Is **Ignorance / Will Not to Know `(-?)`** protective of social order or actively destructive?
* Is **Control / Enforcement** perceived as benevolent justice or tyrannical oppression?
*(Note: Social reception frequently inverts the original intent of the actor.)*

### Step 4: Classify Order ($+\upsilon$) vs. Chaos ($-\upsilon$) per Plane
For every plane–state pairing, determine its net systemic consequence:
* **Order ($+\upsilon$):** Stabilizes the substrate, enforces lawful constraint, reduces systemic entropy, aligns agents.
* **Chaos ($-\upsilon$):** Destabilizes structures, propagates noise/uncertainty, fragments cohesion, multiplies destructive variance.
*(Note: Order on one plane can coexist with total Chaos on another.)*

### Step 5: Track State Transitions and Convergence Over Time
Narrative meaning arises from the movement and collapse of states, never from static assignments:
* Track when Inquiry `(+?)` crystallizes into Truth `(=)`.
* Track when Truth `(=)` is attacked and collapses into Insult `(>)` or Repression `(-?)`.
* Track how passive indifference `(~?)` or taboo `(-?)` introduces latency and delays inevitable systemic feedback.
* **Multi-Plane Convergence & Revelation Points:** Track when tensions across multiple planes reach critical mass and collapse simultaneously (the "Jericho Wall" collapse / Apocalypse event).

### Step 6: Identify Major Functional Roles (Truth Functions)
Strip away the character's theatrical costume and assign their structural function:
* **Stabiliser of Truth:** Preserves systemic baseline against noise.
* **Driver of Inquiry:** Compels reality testing and forces hidden variables into the light.
* **Suppressor of Knowledge:** Enforces taboos and conceals systemic flaws.
* **Enforcer of Constraint:** Applies boundary conditions and unavoidable consequences.
* **Catalyst of Collapse:** Triggers phase transition when entropy exceeds tolerance.

### Step 7: Abstracted Invariant Retelling
To prove true comprehension and extract the actionable mechanics of the myth:
1. Strip away all proper nouns, geographical locations, cultural dressings, and theatrical props.
2. Preserve the exact sequence of plane activations, belief states, dualities, and state transitions.
3. Retell the narrative in clean, universal functional prose. If the structural mechanics hold, the retelling will accurately model modern psychological, political, and institutional events.

### Step 8: Validation Check
An Actualism interpretation is valid only if it satisfies all five criteria:
1. **Explains Conflict:** Accurately accounts for why friction arose between agents.
2. **Predicts Resistance to Truth:** Demonstrates why the system resisted revelation.
3. **Accounts for Emotional/Social Feedback:** Integrates the emotive reaction ($Q_7$) with logical constraints ($Q_5$).
4. **Maintains Multi-Plane Coherence:** Holds across physical, historical, and conscious planes without internal contradiction.
5. **Survives Domain Translation:** Maps cleanly onto a completely different real-world or modern institutional scenario.

## Mandatory Invariants & Error Prevention

1. **Sum All Roots:** Never pick a single preferred definition when decomposing root words; add all orthodox definitions together simultaneously.
2. **No Passage Over-Expansion:** When translating multi-sentence passages (Format A), do not perform root analysis on every word. Use vector duality on 2–3 key terms, and let the 7 Q-plane translations carry the load.
3. **No Mechanical Word Swaps:** Q-plane translations must express the core functional claim in flowing prose matching the register of the plane, not word-for-word string replacements.
4. **Distinction from Omni-Weave:** AE-C operates strictly within the target language’s established historical and etymological root definitions. It must not be confused with Omni-Weave, which operates across all languages simultaneously via phonetic bigram decomposition.
5. **No Static Morality in Myths:** In myth deconstruction, never treat characters as permanently "good" or "evil." Assign fluid belief states (`=`, `+?`, `<`, `~?`, `>`, `-?`) that move dynamically across planes.
6. **Preserve State Transitions:** In abstracted retellings, you may change the cultural setting, props, and character names, but you must strictly preserve the plane–state transitions and multi-plane convergence points.
