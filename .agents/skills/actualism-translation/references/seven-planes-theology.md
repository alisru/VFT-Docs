# The Seven Planes of Reality & Theological Mechanics Reference

Existence is stratified into seven distinct ontological planes. Complete perception and translation requires evaluating all seven modes simultaneously.

## The Seven Planes Architecture

### 1. $Q_1$ Metaphysical / Conscious Plane (WHO)
- **Domain:** Pure will, spiritual authority, prime intentionality, self-reflective agency, conscience.
- **Interrogative:** WHO
- **Canonical Vocabulary:** will, identity, entity, directed, allied, execute, awareness, reflection, sovereignty.
- **Mode of Perception (Eye):** Perfect spiritual vision perceiving the core intent and ultimate source.
- **Conquest Mode (Horn):** The Ascension; the tearing of the veil; unmediated alignment between mind and source.

### 2. $Q_2$ Possible Plane (WHAT)
- **Domain:** Quantum indeterminacy, potentiality, unmanifest blueprints, branching choices, faith, hope.
- **Interrogative:** WHAT
- **Canonical Vocabulary:** probability, variable, data packet, potential, calculate, blueprint, hypothesis.
- **Mode of Perception (Eye):** Faith as the substance of things hoped for, evidence of things not seen.
- **Conquest Mode (Horn):** The Promise; sovereign declaration that turns the impossible into the inevitable.

### 3. $Q_3$ Physical Plane (WHERE)
- **Domain:** Matter, atoms, mass, biological hardware, spatial constraints, kinetic energy.
- **Interrogative:** WHERE
- **Canonical Vocabulary:** physical, material, mass, kinetic, spatial, hardware, biological, substrate.
- **Mode of Perception (Eye):** Seeing the glory of reality expressed directly in the dust/matter.
- **Conquest Mode (Horn):** The Incarnation; the Word becoming flesh, proving matter is the container of spirit.

### 4. $Q_4$ Lyrical Plane (WHY)
- **Domain:** Resonance, frequency, poetry, music, symbolic narrative, intrinsic meaning.
- **Interrogative:** WHY
- **Canonical Vocabulary:** resonance, harmony, melody, soul, frequency, intrinsic meaning, song.
- **Mode of Perception (Eye):** The "New Song"; perceiving the harmonic frequency and acoustic geometry of reality.
- **Conquest Mode (Horn):** The Voice / The Trumpet; acoustic authority that shakes foundations and breaks chains.

### 5. $Q_5$ Logical Plane (HOW)
- **Domain:** Reason, algorithmic causality, formal mathematics, systemic rules, computational constraints.
- **Interrogative:** HOW
- **Canonical Vocabulary:** algorithm, protocol, calculate, system, compute, processing unit, constraint.
- **Mode of Perception (Eye):** Wisdom at the foundation; perceiving structural truth and mathematical consistency.
- **Conquest Mode (Horn):** The Truth; the radical logic of systemic constraint overcoming human sophistry.

### 6. $Q_6$ Historical Plane (CAUSE)
- **Domain:** Time, memory, lineage, temporal sequence, ancestral precedent, irreversible causality.
- **Interrogative:** CAUSE
- **Canonical Vocabulary:** historical, sequence, causal, initiate, timeline, precedent, lineage.
- **Mode of Perception (Eye):** Declaring the end from the beginning; perfect recollection of temporal causality.
- **Conquest Mode (Horn):** The Resurrection; breaking the closed loop of past trauma and deterministic fate.

### 7. $Q_7$ Emotive / Emotional Plane (EFFECT)
- **Domain:** Passion, affect, desire, grief, bliss, interpersonal friction, visceral consequence.
- **Interrogative:** EFFECT
- **Canonical Vocabulary:** emotive, passion, consequence, bliss, resultant, friction, affect, sorrow.
- **Mode of Perception (Eye):** Feeling the uncorrupted pathos and direct emotional reality of beings.
- **Conquest Mode (Horn):** The Passion (Suffering); redeeming sorrow and friction into transformative power.

## The Multi-Plane Collapse (The Seventh Day / Jericho Moment)
When all seven planes are perceived and activated simultaneously without distortion:
- The Physical is experienced as Lyrical.
- The Historical is experienced as Possible.
- The Logical is experienced as Emotive.
- The Metaphysical becomes physically manifest.
All artificial partitions fall flat, revealing undivided Reality.
