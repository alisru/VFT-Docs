# Belief States and Dynamic Transitions Reference

## The Six Personal Belief States

| Symbol | State Name | Operational Definition & Systemic Behavior |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **`(=)`** | **Truth** | Accepted, enforced, or stabilized reality; direct alignment with facts and structural feedback. |
| **`(+?)`** | **Will to Know** | Active inquiry, testing, empirical exploration, seeking systemic signal and hidden variables. |
| **`(<)`** | **Lie** | Active distortion, manipulation, false projection, concealment of signal, predatory framing. |
| **`(~?)`** | **Indifferent** | Neglect, passive neutrality, entropic drift, apathy toward signal, uncalibrated stasis. |
| **`(>)`** | **Insult** | Direct attack, hostile domination, destabilization, aggressive invalidation of agents or structures. |
| **`(-?)`** | **Will Not to Know** | Active denial, repression, enforcement of taboo, willful blindness, structural censorship. |

## Dynamic Transition Mechanics

Meaning is generated by state transitions under systemic constraint, not static assignments:

1. **Inquiry Crystallization:** `(+?)` $\to$ `(=)`. An agent tests hypotheses or explores constraints until systemic reality is verified and integrated.
2. **Defensive Collapse into Invalidation:** `(=)` $\to$ `(>)` or `(-?)`. When a system or ego encounters an unbearable truth, it frequently collapses into active attack `(>)` or institutional taboo `(-?)`.
3. **Latency and Delay:** `(~?)` and `(-?)` introduce temporal drag into a system. They delay inevitable feedback loops until physical or logical collapse is unavoidable.
4. **Multi-Plane Convergence / Revelation (The Jericho Moment):** When belief states across multiple planes (e.g. Physical, Logical, Conscious) simultaneously align with Truth `(=)` or exceed the threshold of contradiction, institutional barriers collapse completely.

## The Five Truth Functions

Strip character names and cultural costumes to assign structural roles:
- **Stabiliser of Truth:** Preserves systemic baseline against entropic drift.
- **Driver of Inquiry:** Compels reality testing and brings unobserved variables to light.
- **Suppressor of Knowledge:** Enforces taboos and conceals systemic contradiction.
- **Enforcer of Constraint:** Executes boundary conditions and delivers unavoidable systemic feedback.
- **Catalyst of Collapse:** Triggers phase transition when entropy exceeds system tolerance.
