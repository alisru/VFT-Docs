# Coordinate System (υ, ψ)

## Axis υ (Morality — Who benefits)
+2.0  Everyone / All Beings (Systemic Justice)
+1.0  Other People (Greater Good)
 0.0  Neutral
-1.0  My Group Only (Lesser Evil)
-2.0  Only Me (Tyranny / Pure Extraction)

Spectrum: Everyone > Other Beings > A Being > No One > My Group > Me > Only Me

## Axis ψ (Will — What the energy is doing)
+2.0  Actively creating systemic value for all (Productive Justice)
+1.0  Proactive (creating, building, acting)
 0.0  Neutral (stasis)
-1.0  Passive (allowing, suppressing, withholding)
-2.0  Actively destroying or extracting value (Chaos / Collapse)

## Modifier Rules
- WHERE modifies υ: energy injected into a structurally corrupt or decaying domain pulls υ toward negative regardless of stated intent.
- HOW modifies ψ: coercive or deceptive method forces ψ negative regardless of stated purpose.

## Zone Classifications
Corner anchors at +/-1:
- Greater Good (+1, +1)
- Greatest Lie (-1, +1)
- Lesser Good (+1, -1)
- Greater Evil (-1, -1)

Full zones:
- Greater Good: (+υ, +ψ)
- Greatest Lie: (-υ, +ψ)
- Lesser Good: (+υ, -ψ)
- Greater Evil: (-υ, -ψ)
- Tension Point: mixed or contested vectors
- Constraint: neutral / environmental fact

## Perceptual Inversion Warning (The 0.5 Zone)
Near the self-end of the spectrum, ego distorts perception.
Extraction feels like Strength. Service feels like Burden. Stability feels like Rot. Cruelty feels like Protection.
When an actor's stated moral position conflicts with the calculated coordinate, flag this distortion explicitly.
