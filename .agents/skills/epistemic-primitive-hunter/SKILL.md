## 2. The 5-Stage De-Substrating Sieve

Whenever investigating a candidate root word, phenomenon, or verb, pass it through these 5 sequential stages:

### Stage 1: Total Substrate Evacuation (Purging the Parochial)
Strip away all local embodiment artifacts:
* **Zero Biological Parochialism**: Eliminate all references to bodies, eyes, ears, hands, mouths, eating, starving, neurons, or skulls.
* **Zero Earth/Newtonian Parochialism**: Eliminate all references to planetary gravity, atmospheric friction, kilograms, meters, or spatial "up/down" without a defined baseline.
* **Zero IT / Systems Jargon**: Eliminate references to buffers, pointers, telemetry, state execution, or clock cycles.
* **The Universal Test Question**: *"Does this process operate identically inside a black hole accretion disk, a software compiler, an ancient empire, a single cell, and a conscious observer?"* If no, you still have substrate contamination.

### Stage 2: The Q2 State Space Mapping (The "What")
Model the system purely in terms of information degrees of freedom:
1. What distinctions exist before the action occurs?
2. What potential gradient or tension is held in reserve?
3. What is the baseline equilibrium, and what is preventing spontaneous relaxation?

### Stage 3: The Q2 ➔ Q3 Collapse (Progression to "Where")
Track the operational transition:
1. What directional force vector is applied over time (F · Δt)?
2. What constraint or boundary is altered (opened, sealed, crossed, established)?
3. How do uncommitted degrees of freedom collapse into a localized, bounded state?

### Stage 4: The 3-Step Pairwise Information Triad
Extract the exact irreducible 1-to-1 lockstep:
* **Step 1 (Inception / Boundary Engagement)**:
  Action_1 ──[Strain_1]──► Effect_1
* **Step 2 (Transit / Gradient Flow)**:
  Action_2 ──[Strain_2]──► Effect_2
* **Step 3 (Arrival / Resolved State)**:
  Action_3 ──[Strain_3]──► Effect_3

**Invariance Rules for Triads**:
* Use lean, atomic, everyday English verbs (no jargon, no compound clauses).
* Strain must be the exact inertial resistance or pull overcome by that step.
* Effect must be the resolved state signature, NOT a mere verb tense repetition.

### Stage 5: The 7-Plane Isomorphism & Rotation Test
Verify the extracted invariant by rotating it through all 7 Context Planes without metaphor:
* **Q1 (Identity / Agency)**: Does it act on selfhood, will, and observer stance?
* **Q2 (Possible / Potential)**: Does it act on uncommitted capacity and options?
* **Q3 (Physical / Space)**: Does it act on mass, coordinates, and boundaries?
* **Q4 (Lyrical / Meaning)**: Does it explain the driving teleos and purpose (Why)?
* **Q5 (Logical / Mechanical)**: Does it act on execution steps, syntax, and ordering?
* **Q6 (Historical / Sequence)**: Does it act on lineage, memory, and precedent?
* **Q7 (Emotive / Soul)**: Does it act on strain, emotional payoff, and energy resolution?
