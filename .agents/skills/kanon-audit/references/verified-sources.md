## Key Verified Quotes

"I consider myself just an ordinary Australian who wants to keep this great country strong and independent, and my greatest desire is to see all Australians treat each other as equals as we travel together towards the new century." -MS96

"I and most Australians want our immigration policy radically reviewed and that of multiculturalism abolished." -MS96

"Along with millions of Australians, I am fed up to the back teeth with the inequalities that are being promoted by the government and paid for by the taxpayer under the assumption that Aboriginals are the most disadvantaged people in Australia." -MS96

"Present governments are encouraging separatism in Australia by providing opportunities, land, moneys and facilities available only to Aboriginals." -MS96

"If politicians continue to promote separatism in Australia, they should not continue to hold their seats in this parliament. They are not truly representing all Australians." -MS96

"Like most Australians, I worked for my land; no-one gave it to me." -MS96

"At the end of World War II Australia's population was over seven million people and 90% of those people were born in Australia. In 1945 we were short of labour... But today we are a population of 25 million people and there is no need to have the highest level of legal migration in the world." -SON18b

"No, I won't and never will." -WTC22

"We are a nation of migrants. Migrants have built this country and migrants will continue to build this country." -MS16

"Islam is a totalitarian, political ideology masquerading as a religion." -SON18a

## If No Direct Quote Exists

Use a documented paraphrase or description of a documented action. Format:
[Paraphrase: description of documented position/action] -Source (year)

Never invent a quote. Never composite two real quotes into one. Never attribute a paraphrase without marking it as such.
