Community Welfare Survey Instrument (Dual-Vector $7 \times 7$ Matrix Edition)
Based on the $7 \times 4$ Survival Matrix Framework
1. Executive Overview & Methodology

The $7 \times 4$ Survival Matrix Assessment Tool evaluates total community viability ($V$) across Seven Core Survival Requirements:

Recognition (Metaphysical Plane, $c_1$): Identity, visibility, dignity, and skill utilization.

Purpose (Possible Plane, $c_2$): Trajectory space, future options, mobility, and career pathways.

Shelter (Physical Plane, $c_3$): Physical safety, spatial containment, thermoregulation, and infrastructure.

Refreshment (Lyrical Plane, $c_4$): Restorative rest, aesthetic joy, play, and spiritual renewal.

Power (Logical Plane, $c_5$): Executive agency, governance, democratic access, and decision-making authority.

Morale (Historical Plane, $c_6$): Internal spirit, conviction, historical truth, fortitude, and resilience.

Sustenance (Emotive Plane, $c_7$): Nervous system safety, emotional climate, mutual care, and social cohesion.

Survey Architecture: The Dual-Vector $(A, B)$ Format

Every item in this instrument evaluates a two-dimensional vector pairing Sense (Capacity / Potential) with Use (Execution / Realization):

$Item
p,i
	​

=[A (Sense): Availability, perception, or capacity]AND[B (Use): Active deployment, execution, or impact]$


Evaluating both dimensions reveals critical structural failure modes:

High $A$, Low $B$ (Suppression / Latent Bottleneck): High latent capacity, skills, or awareness that are suppressed, ignored, or blocked from active realization.

Low $A$, High $B$ (Coercive Duty / Empty Action): Action forced upon an individual without underlying agency, security, or self-sense.

High $A$, High $B$ (Coherent Flourishing): Full alignment between potential and real-world execution.

Scale & Rating Standard

Each statement vector is rated on a 1 to 5 scale:

$1=Non-Existent / Severe Deprivation (0.00)2=Unstable / Marginal (0.25)3=Adequate Baseline (0.50)$ $4=Secure / Strong (0.75)5=Flourishing / Fully Realized (1.00)$
