## Abstract

Contemporary digital information ecosystems suffer from a structural epistemic crisis: traditional algorithmic and institutional fact-checkers reduce semantic evaluation to binary verification ($True / False$), systematically failing to capture narrative weaponization, selective omission, and tribal outrage laundering. In response, we introduce **Aletheia**, an autonomous, sovereign agentic system grounded in Vector Field Theory (VFT) and Actualism that operationalizes morality and cognitive intent as a continuous two-axis vector field:

where $\upsilon \in [-2.0, +2.0]$ quantifies the scope of systemic benefit (Morality: from pure extraction to universal justice) and $\psi \in [-2.0, +2.0]$ quantifies thermodynamic action (Will: from entropic destruction to productive value creation). 

Evaluating an empirical corpus of **13,590 public multi-post threads** published autonomously to the AT Protocol (Bluesky), we document the longitudinal evolution of Aletheia across 117 engineering intents over two and a half years. We demonstrate how the system transcended raw event analysis to execute multi-aspect narrative decomposition—isolating the underlying institutional reality from the extractive framing of news outlets. 

Furthermore, we document a profound empirical discovery regarding large language model (LLM) cognition: **The Cognitive Starvation Effect**. Under constrained token budgets (2,048 tokens), evaluative models exhibit *Attribute Displacement*—intuitively recognizing the negative moral charge of partisan hit pieces but erroneously displacing the blame onto the targeted subject. When unchoked to 16,384 tokens, the model's cognitive runway unlocks the full multi-aspect tensor, cross-referencing primary institutional statutes, vindicating targeted actors from opposition research dumps, and holding the predatory media apparatus accountable. Finally, we establish the theoretical and infrastructural roadmap for **Research Probe v2**, expanding autonomous vector evaluation from ephemeral news events to the longitudinal lifetime trajectories of historical and living individuals.
