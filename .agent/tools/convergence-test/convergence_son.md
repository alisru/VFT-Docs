## Phase 2 — Vector Verification (SON Method)

Instead of qualitative coordinate assignment, the (u, ψ) coordinate is calculated as the **equilibrium point of vector forces** exerted by the six attractors of the Psochic Hegemony.

### The (p·t + n) Moral Primitive (Grounding Definition)

**p is the probability mass distribution over future states, t is time, and n represents latent potential/capacity.** The test measures whether the net possibility-time space is expanding or contracting:
*   **Good** = Δ(p·t + n) > 0 (passive) → probability mass/potential shifts toward better futures naturally.
*   **Bad** = Δ(p·t + n) < 0 (passive) → probability mass/potential decays toward worse futures by neglect.
*   **Evil** = Δ(p·t + n) < 0 (active, premature) → probability mass/potential is actively forced to contract, extinguishing better futures.
*   **Saintly** = Δ(p·t + n) > 0 (active, under collapse pressure) → probability mass/potential is actively held open against active closure forces.

### 1. The 6 Attractor Fields (A_i)

#### The 4 Moral Attractors (The ±1.0 Corner Ring)
These represent active/passive moral and volitional forces:
*   **Greater Good (A_GG)** = (+1.0, +1.0)
*   **Greater Evil (A_GE)** = (-1.0, -1.0)
*   **Lesser Good (A_LG)** = (+1.0, -1.0)
*   **Lesser Evil / Greatest Lie (A_LE)** = (-1.0, +1.0)

#### The 2 Preferences (Neutral Will Attractors)
These represent localized horizontal forces of subjective preference (neutral will, ψ = 0.0):
*   **Good Preference (A_GP)** = (+1.0, 0.0)
*   **Bad Preference (A_BP)** = (-1.0, 0.0)

For the preference attractors, **Support (S)** is a neutral-will preference, **Oppose (O)** drives Will in the opposite direction (suppressing GP drives Will negative; correcting/opposing BP drives Will positive), and **Neutral (N)** acts as pure weight dilution contributing 0.0 force vector pull.

For each attractor i, score the forces in the range [0.0, 2.0] based on evidence:
*   **Support (S_i)**: *Attraction* — the degree to which the action reinforces the attractor.
*   **Oppose (O_i)**: *Repulsion* — the degree to which the action actively violates or conflicts with the attractor.
*   **Neutral (N_i)**: *Orbit* (for moral attractors) or *Dilution* (for preferences) — the degree to which the action is indifferent, orthogonal, or independent of the attractor.

#### The Gravity Multiplier Scale ([0.0, 2.0]):
*   **0.0**: No force / Neutral (no alignment).
*   **1.0**: Condition Met (standard expected value in the **Human Horizon**, e.g., others/proactive).
*   **2.0**: Totality of Exceedence (systemic/compounding impact in the **Systemic Horizon**, representing the maximum possible force).
    *   *Note:* A score of 2.0 exerts double the gravitational pull of a standard 1.0 force, allowing total-exceedence forces to dominate and pull the equilibrium coordinate toward their anchor.

### 2. Orthogonal (Orbit) Vectors (A_i_perp)
For the 4 Moral Attractors, the orthogonal orbit vector is A_i_perp = (-ψ_i, u_i):
*   A_GG_perp = (-1.0, +1.0)
*   A_GE_perp = (+1.0, -1.0)
*   A_LG_perp = (+1.0, +1.0)
*   A_LE_perp = (-1.0, -1.0)

*(Note: Perpendicular orbit vectors do not apply to preference attractors GP/BP, which have no orthogonal force contribution.)*

### 3. Equilibrium Coordinate Calculation (C_net / 18-Variable Parameter Space)
To maintain structural resolution and prevent coordinate collapse, **every single one of the 6 attractors MUST have its full [S_i, O_i, N_i] triple scored** (18 variables in total). Scoring only a single dominant aspect per point is a methodological error.

1. **Calculate Net Attractor Force (F_i) for each attractor:**
   * For the 4 Moral Attractors (GG, GE, LG, LE):
     F_i = S_i * A_i - O_i * A_i + N_i * A_i_perp
   * For Good Preference (GP):
     F_GP = S_GP * (1.0, 0.0) - O_GP * (1.0, 1.0)
   * For Bad Preference (BP):
     F_BP = S_BP * (-1.0, 0.0) - O_BP * (-1.0, -1.0)

2. **Morality Coordinate (u):**
   Sum and normalize the u-components of the forces across all 6 attractors:
   u = sum(F_i_u) / sum(S_i + O_i + N_i)

3. **Will Coordinate (ψ) — Separated Will (Like-Type) Protocol:**
   To prevent positive narrative spin (S_LE) and actual physical destruction (S_GE) from cancelling each other out to a neutral stasis (ψ ≈ 0), calculate positive and negative Will forces independently:
   *   Group forces by Will sign:
       *   Ψ_pos = {i | F_i_ψ > 0}
       *   Ψ_neg = {i | F_i_ψ < 0}
   *   Sum absolute force magnitudes for each direction:
       *   F_pos = sum(|F_j_ψ|) for j in Ψ_pos
       *   F_neg = sum(|F_k_ψ|) for k in Ψ_neg
   *   Identify the dominant direction and calculate ψ using ONLY that dominant group:
       *   If F_pos >= F_neg: Net Will is positive (constructive/deceptive):
           ψ = sum(F_j_ψ) / sum(S_j + O_j + N_j) for j in Ψ_pos
       *   If F_neg > F_pos: Net Will is negative (destructive/suppressing):
           ψ = sum(F_k_ψ) / sum(S_k + O_k + N_k) for k in Ψ_neg

4. **Ambiguity Volume (z) & z-profile**:
   Blanks do not damage coherence. They represent open potential:
   *   z = sum of all B counts across all 7 Q planes (max z = 49)
   *   z-profile = [B_Q1, B_Q2, B_Q3, B_Q4, B_Q5, B_Q6, B_Q7]
   *   Report local z at each sub-judgement depth.

5. **Vector Coherence Magnitude (V_Qn)**:
   Calculated per plane (blanks excluded):
   V_Qn = sum(P_magnitudes) / (sum(P_magnitudes) + n_F)
   (If P + F = 0, V_Qn = 1.0)

6. **Integrity Score (R_net)**:
   R_net = 1 / (V_Q1 * V_Q2 * V_Q3 * V_Q4 * V_Q5 * V_Q6 * V_Q7)
   *   1.0 = structurally coherent truth
   *   1.0 - 2.0 = minor gaps, conditionally sound
   *   2.0 - 10.0 = meaningful distortion
   *   10.0 - 100.0 = significant failure
   *   100.0+ = structurally tyrannical claim

7. **Object State Blanking Protocol**:
   When the subject is an inanimate object, raw tool, or concept without an operator:
   *   Auto-blank: Q1 WHO, Q4 WHY, Q5 HOW, Q7 EFFECT -> all B (28 z points)
   *   Lock/Cap: Q3 WHERE and Q6 CAUSE (maker's imprint), Q2 WHAT (physical bounds) -> P/F
   *   Default Object state: z = 28, z-profile = [7, 0, 0, 7, 7, 0, 7]
