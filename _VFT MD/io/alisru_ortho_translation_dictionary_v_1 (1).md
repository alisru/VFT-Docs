# Alisru ↔ OrthoGPT Translation Dictionary

## Purpose
This dictionary maps:
- common orthodox English interpretations
- against the process-relative / systems-relative meanings used in the Alisru framework.

The goal is not to redefine language universally.
The goal is to reduce semantic drift when discussing:
- systems
- morality
- economics
- power
- coordination
- geopolitics
- behaviour
- incentives
- emergence
- constraint architectures

---

# Core Meta-Rules

## 1. Most labels are process labels, not identity labels
In the Alisru framework:
- words usually describe active behavioural processes
- not permanent identities or essences.

Example:
- "selfish" does not mean "evil person"
- it means behaviour routing value/control toward narrower self-benefit at the expense of broader stability.

Likewise:
- "altruistic" is not sainthood
- it is behaviour routing value toward broader mutual/system benefit.

Actors can transition between states.

---

## 2. Behaviour exists on vectors and spectra
Almost no category is binary.
Most concepts are:
- continuous
- layered
- contextual
- dynamic
- emergent

The framework prefers:
- gradients
- vectors
- state transitions
- probability densities
- attractor regions

instead of rigid categorical boxes.

---

## 3. Outcomes matter more than declarations
Intent matters.
But:
- systems are judged heavily through predictable outcomes
- structural effects
- incentive flows
- and emergent behavioural consequences.

A declared intention does not override a consistently extractive process.

# Dictionary

---

# Altruistic

## Orthodox meaning
Selflessly caring for others, often at personal cost.
Usually interpreted morally.

## Alisru-relative meaning
A process orientation where:
- behaviour routes value toward broader mutual/system benefit
- preserves cooperative stability
- expands collective capability or survivability
- reduces destructive extraction pressure.

Not a permanent identity.
Not necessarily passive or weak.
Can involve force when preserving broader stability.

---

# Selfish

## Orthodox meaning
Excessively concerned with oneself.
Greedy or inconsiderate.

## Alisru-relative meaning
A process orientation where:
- behaviour routes value/control toward narrower self-preservation or self-expansion
- often externalizes costs onto others
- increases extractive pressure
- reduces broader system balance.

Not permanent.
Not inherently evil.
Can be adaptive in some environments.

---

# Evil

## Orthodox meaning
Morally wrong, wicked, malicious.

## Alisru-relative meaning
A sustained process of knowingly destructive or extractive behaviour that:
- unnecessarily damages broader systems
- collapses cooperative possibility space
- increases suffering or destabilization for gain.

Usually tied more to persistent process than isolated action.

---

# Good

## Orthodox meaning
Morally correct, virtuous.

## Alisru-relative meaning
Processes that:
- preserve or improve broader system survivability
- expand healthy possibility space
- support sustainable coordination
- minimize unnecessary destructive externalities.

---

# Freedom

## Orthodox meaning
Liberty, lack of restriction.

## Alisru-relative meaning
The degree of viable accessible possibility-space available to an actor.

Includes:
- capability
- optionality
- autonomy
- survivability
- access to meaningful action.

Not merely absence of rules.

---

# Power

## Orthodox meaning
Control, authority, strength, influence.

## Alisru-relative meaning
The capacity to:
- shape constraint fields
- alter incentive landscapes
- influence probability distributions of outcomes
- redirect system flows
- modify accessible possibility-space.

Power is often indirect.

---

# Control

## Orthodox meaning
Direct command over others.

## Alisru-relative meaning
Shaping environments, incentives, constraints, and dependencies such that:
- actors predictably move toward preferred outcomes
without requiring explicit command.

"Leaning on strings" rather than directly pulling them.

---

# Authoritarianism

## Orthodox meaning
Centralized coercive government.

## Alisru-relative meaning
A system drift where:
- freedom/possibility-space is increasingly traded for centralized stability, predictability, or power concentration.

Can emerge structurally under:
- scarcity
- instability
- dependency
- fear
- coordination failure.

Not necessarily binary dictatorship.

---

# Cooperation

## Orthodox meaning
Working together.

## Alisru-relative meaning
Distributed coordination of effort/resources toward mutually beneficial outcomes.

Can exist:
- voluntarily
- institutionally
- transactionally
- strategically
- or emergently.

---

# Exploitation

## Orthodox meaning
Unfair use of others.

## Alisru-relative meaning
Extractive asymmetry where:
- value flows disproportionately toward one actor
while:
- costs, restrictions, or instability are externalized.

---

# Dependency

## Orthodox meaning
Reliance.

## Alisru-relative meaning
Constraint attachment.

A condition where:
- survival
- stability
- prosperity
- or functionality
requires continued access to an external node/system.

Dependencies create leverage surfaces.

---

# Hegemony

## Orthodox meaning
Dominance by one state/group.

## Alisru-relative meaning
A dominant constraint-anchor within a system.

The actor/system most capable of:
- shaping incentives
- defining legitimacy
- enforcing costs
- rewarding alignment
- and structuring global behavioural flows.

- distributed coordination
- emergent order
- and structural pressure fields.
