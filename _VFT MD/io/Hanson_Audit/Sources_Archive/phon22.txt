---EXTRACTED TEXT (full post caption)---

"Recently I had the opportunity to speak at the Australian Monarchist League conference about why I believe Australia would be wise to keep our constitutional monarchy. It may not be fashionable to say so, and it may even seem counter-intuitive, but I am a constitutional monarchist because I understand a simple and undeniable truth: the monarchy is an essential foundation of Australian democracy and sovereignty. Our Constitution is a great achievement, reflecting the establishment of our great nation. People from distant colonies gathered together to debate and draft a Constitution which has served Australia very well over the past 122 years. [...] I am, of course, talking about the proposal to recognise indigenous traditional ownership of Australia in the Constitution and the proposal to enshrine an indigenous 'voice to Parliament' in the Constitution. I am firmly opposed to these proposals as well. Indeed, One Nation will be leading a strong campaign for the 'NO' vote in the coming referendum for these proposals. It took 66 years for our Constitution to be made colour-blind, and there is no compelling reason why it should be regressed to again single out a number of Australians for different treatment based on their race. [...] The founding principles of our nation, those enshrined in our constitution and our institutions of law and government, must be protected from those who want them destroyed, forgotten and replaced with something much worse."
