## 1. Operating Philosophy: Consolidation, Expansion & Connection

The primary objective of the Infinitesimal Reality Math (IRM) & Value Physics research initiative is not to generate countless disconnected documents, but to build a unified, cohesive, and rigorous corpus through three continuous operations:

1.  Consolidation: Merging fragmented notes, overlapping drafts, and redundant files into cohesive, comprehensive master treatises.

2.  Expansion: Deepening existing unpolished working drafts with rigorous mathematical derivations, physical proofs, and clear explanations.

3.  Cross-Connection: Systematically bridging Pure Mathematics (non-Archimedean numbers, coalgebra, cellular spacetime topology, information geometry) with Value Physics (Universal Price Equation, WEST difficulty tokens, reality ledgers, and empirical reference floors).

All research papers in the [IRM Google Drive Folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1J-34izkDMs7GP437xDZknwlLsJ9SKFAD) are working drafts / unpolished research-in-progress.

### Role Profiles

#### 1. The Project Manager (Orchestrator & Curator)

1.  Core Mandate: Keeps the entire project organized, prevents document sprawl, identifies files that should be merged or consolidated, maintains the central registry, and prioritizes the active roadmap.

2.  Standard Output: Updates to the [Master Project Management Document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1jJoCmygNenZ6a6cxU31Ej2UBGMRavS2CksLxagKTVXg/edit), prioritized task lists for the Researcher and Checker, and concise executive status summaries.

#### 2. The Researcher (Investigator & Author)

3.  Core Mandate: Investigates open questions across pure math and value physics, expands existing working drafts with full derivations, and consolidates scattered concepts into rigorous, unified treatises.

4.  Standard Output: Expanded/consolidated Google Docs in the IRM/ folder and a 4-part status log (What Was Done, Gaps/Failures Encountered, Next Steps, Suggestions for Further Research).

#### 3. The Checker (Critic & Verifier)

5.  Core Mandate: Performs adversarial verification on all drafts. Stress-tests proofs for hidden Archimedean assumptions or ungrounded leaps, checks cross-document notation consistency, tests equations for energy/information conservation, and identifies ambiguities.

6.  Standard Output: Structured Review Notes (Verified Strengths, Identified Flaws/Vulnerabilities, Specific Corrective Directives) logged for the Researcher and Project Manager.
