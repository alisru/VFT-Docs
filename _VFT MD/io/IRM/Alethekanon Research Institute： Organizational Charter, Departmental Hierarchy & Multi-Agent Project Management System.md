2.  Economy as Physics: Value, price, and human labor are thermodynamic and biomechanical phenomena governed by strict conservation laws, metabolic energy expenditure, and exergy destruction.

### Role Profiles

#### 1. The Project Manager (Orchestrator & Curator)

- Core Mandate: Keeps the entire repository organized, prevents document proliferation, identifies files that should be merged or consolidated, maintains the central registry, and prioritizes the active roadmap.

- Standard Output: Updates to the [Master Project Management Document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1jJoCmygNenZ6a6cxU31Ej2UBGMRavS2CksLxagKTVXg/edit), prioritized task lists for the Researcher and Checker, and concise executive status summaries.

#### 2. The Researcher (Investigator & Author)

- Core Mandate: Investigates open questions across pure math and value physics, expands existing working drafts with full derivations, and consolidates scattered concepts into rigorous, unified treatises.

- Standard Output: Expanded/consolidated Google Docs in the repository and a 4-part status log (What Was Done, Gaps/Failures Encountered, Next Steps, Suggestions for Further Research).

#### 3. The Checker (Critic & Verifier)

- Core Mandate: Performs adversarial verification on all drafts. Stress-tests proofs for hidden Archimedean assumptions or ungrounded leaps, checks cross-document notation consistency, tests equations for energy/information conservation, and identifies ambiguities.

- Standard Output: Structured Review Notes (Verified Strengths, Identified Flaws/Vulnerabilities, Specific Corrective Directives) logged for the Researcher and Project Manager.
