# Executive Summary

Traditional regional economic models assume homogeneous spatial development and rely on centralized fiat debt financing to clear trade balances. Under asymmetric climate hazards (localized droughts, floods, or bushfires) and external monetary shocks (central bank rate tightening), regional economies experience severe liquidity drainage, bankruptcy contagion, and ecological asset stripping.

This treatise presents a 10-node dynamic network simulation of interconnected regional communities across a 156-week operational horizon, modeling 6,500 total citizens across the Warrumbungle-Orana bioregion in New South Wales, Australia. We formalize the continuous-time Dynamic Leontief-Differential System (\$\\frac{d\\mathbf{x}(t)}{dt} = (\\mathbf{I} - \\mathbf{A}(t))\\mathbf{x}(t) - \\mathbf{c}(t)\$) and the Inter-Sovereign Trade Tensor (\$\\mathbf{\\Xi}\_{\\text{trade}}\$) to govern multi-commodity resource exchanges (Food, Energy, Water, Shelter, Care).

Under compound stressors—including a 30-week severe drought localized to northern agrarian nodes (50% yield reduction), a +65% global diesel fuel shock (\$2.40/L), and a +425 bps central bank interest rate hike—the simulation demonstrates:

1.  **Neoclassical Debt Fragility:** Centralized commercial banking mechanisms trigger widespread municipal and farm insolvencies (\$10.0 / 10.0\$ default rate), expanding cumulative debt to **\$179.5M AUD** and causing **\$878.7k in unhedged ecological carrying capacity (\$R_n\$) destruction**.

2.  **Value Physics Network Solvency:** Operating under Theorem 5 (Statutory Debt Bifurcation at 0.0% interest), statutory disaster amortization pauses, and Book 2 shared strategic reserve transfers, the network maintains **100% solvency (\$P\_{\\text{default}} = 0.000\$)**, steadily pays down principal debt linearly to **\$147.0M AUD**, accumulates **\$42.0M WEST equivalents in collective emergency reserves**, and expands regional carrying capacity by **\$+8.79M units**.

## 2.1 The Regularized Inter-Sovereign Trade Tensor (\$\\mathbf{\\Xi}\_{\\text{trade}}\$)

\$\$\\mathbf{\\Xi}*{\\text{trade}}(i, j, t) = \\frac{\\text{WEST}*{\\text{exported}}(i, t)}{\\text{WEST}*{\\text{imported}}(j, t)} \\cdot \\left\[ \\frac{R*{n,i}(t) + \\Delta R\_{n,i}(t)}{R\_{n,j}(t) + \\Delta R\_{n,j}(t)} \\right\] \\cdot \\exp\\left( -\\frac{d\_{ij}}{L_0} \\cdot \\frac{P\_{3}(t)}{P\_{3,0}} \\right)\$\$

Where:

- \$d\_{ij}\$ is the spatial Euclidean distance between nodes (ranging from \$15\\text{ km}\$ to \$136\\text{ km}\$).

- \$L_0 = 100\\text{ km}\$ is the characteristic regional transport decay scale.

- \$P_3(t) / P\_{3,0}\$ is the real fuel price friction multiplier.

- The carrying capacity ratio \$\\frac{R\_{n,i} + \\Delta R\_{n,i}}{R\_{n,j} + \\Delta R\_{n,j}}\$ ensures that resource-depleting exports are automatically taxed while ecological regeneration earns an exchange premium.

# 5. Key Empirical Findings

1.  **Elimination of Bankruptcy Cascades:** Under Neoclassical debt compounding, when drought hit Nodes 1, 3, and 5, their revenue collapsed while interest obligations doubled. To service bank loans, they were forced to cut maintenance and draw emergency loans, eventually defaulting and dragging downstream logistics and care hubs into liquidity freezes. Under Value Physics, the **Statutory Disaster Pause Covenant** instantly suspended principal amortization, while Book 2 emergency grain and water buffers maintained continuous community nutrition and clinical operations.

2.  **Spatial Trade Stability via \$\\mathbf{\\Xi}\_{\\text{trade}}\$:** The Inter-Sovereign Trade Tensor prevented predatory terms-of-trade shifts during the drought. Water and grain exported from southern nodes were exchanged at exact physical difficulty parity, preventing panic hoarding.
