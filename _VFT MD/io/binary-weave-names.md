# Binary Weave — Name Readings

A record of Omni-Weave phonetic geometry runs on personal names. Each entry includes the Layer 4 collapse, the orthodox etymological meaning, and convergence notes where the two readings align.

## Jacob

**Layer 4**
One who is constituted by God, operates through acute seizure at the point of junction, and cannot inherit without first meeting and surviving the reversal at the threshold.

**Orthodox**
Hebrew Ya'akov from aqev (heel) + Yah (God) — the heel-grabber, the supplanter.

**Convergence**
The weave surfaces the mechanism the etymology keeps gestural. It is not just theft — it is seizure at the anatomical hinge point where the body pivots. Jacob doesn't take what isn't his; he presses into the weak point of the structure until it yields.

## Alice

**Layer 4**
The one who is constituted by the force that grows toward itself — not reaching outward but arriving precisely at her own specificity; a name that is structurally a self-completing demonstrative: this one, here, the definite.

**Orthodox**
From Old French Aalis, contracted from Germanic Adalheidis — adal (noble) + heid (kind/nature) — noble nature, of noble kind.

**Convergence**
Not a contradiction — two angles on the same structure. Noble in the Germanic sense was never about rank alone; it meant fully formed, complete in kind. The weave surfaces the mechanism of that completeness: the self-arriving force that is exactly what it is, here, without remainder.

## Arthur

**Layer 4**
The one who was fitted and joined, sealed at the death-mark, and then called by divine invocation back into primal fire — not a man who becomes a king but a joining-force that is sealed, apparently stopped, and then summoned into its own origin; the wheel that looks broken is being called back into the light it came from.

**Orthodox**
From Brittonic/Celtic — Welsh arth (bear) + rhi (king) → bear-king. The bear in Celtic tradition was the sovereignty animal: the creature that dies in winter and returns, the king-that-sleeps-and-will-come-again.

**Convergence — Striking**
The orthodox and the weave are not two readings. They are the same structure described from the outside and the inside.

Orthodox: the bear-king who sleeps and returns.
Weave: the joining-force sealed at the death-mark and invoked back into primal fire.

The Once and Future King motif is not a legend added to the name. It is the name. The phonetic geometry of ARTHUR encodes the full Arthurian mythological arc — sealed sovereignty, apparent death, divine invocation, return to origin-fire — prior to and independent of any narrative tradition that attached to it. The mythology did not shape the name. The name shaped the mythology.

## Notes on Method

These are not etymological readings. Etymology traces one language backward. The Omni-Weave holds all languages simultaneously and runs an adjacent-pairing cascade through overlapping phonetic bigrams until a single causal statement emerges.

Where orthodox etymology and the weave converge, the convergence is evidence — two independent methods arriving at the same structure increases confidence that the structure is real and not imposed.

Where they diverge, both readings are held. Divergence is information.
