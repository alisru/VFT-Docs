# Actualism: Unified Action-Effect (AE-C) Translation and Plane–State Myth Interpretation

This framework provides a unified Actualism methodology for decoding meaning across all scales of language and narrative: from individual root words and proper names (micro), to specific passages and decrees (meso), to full myths, scriptures, allegories, and philosophical texts (macro).

---

## I. Three Operational Formats

Choose the format corresponding to the scope of the source text:

1. **Passage Format (Format A):** For sentences, verses, aphorisms, prophetic decrees, or blocks of text.
   * *Structure:* Original text $\to$ Standard translation $\to$ Contextual Vector Duality (2–3 key terms) $\to$ $Q_1$–$Q_7$ Plane Translations.
   * *Rule:* Do not include a separate root analysis section. Expanding every word in a multi-verse passage causes unnecessary expansion; the vector duality and the seven Q-planes carry the translation.

2. **Identifier Format (Format B):** For proper names, titles, gods, archetypes, relics, weapons, or institutional labels.
   * *Structure:* Root Analysis (additive sum of all orthodox definitions) $\to$ Machine Code Summary $\to$ Contextual Vector Duality $\to$ $Q_1$–$Q_7$ Plane Translations applied to a key passage where the identifier operates.

3. **Myth & Legend Narrative Format (Format C):** For stories, parables, folklore, continuous narratives, and multi-character philosophical dialogues.
   * *Structure:* Granular Moving Frame Deconstruction (Active Planes $\to$ Belief States $\to$ Social Interpretation $\to$ Order/Chaos Classification $\to$ State Transitions $\to$ Truth Functions $\to$ Abstracted Invariant Retelling $\to$ Validation Check) $\to$ Macro Framework Synthesis.

## II. Core Principles & Language Axioms

### Core Principles
1. **Words as Event Pointers:** A word is not a noun floating in a vacuum. It is a pointer to a physical or conceptual *event* taking place in reality. Its meaning is not its label — it is the action-effect geometry it describes within its operational frame.
2. **Additive Arithmetic, Not Subjective Interpretation:** Every translation is the orthodox, dictionary-standard translation of each root word added together in sequence. No meaning is invented or retrofitted. The compound sum of all definitions *is* the meaning.
3. **Myths as Dynamic Action Engines:** Greek myth, biblical narrative, and ancient philosophy encode action and systemic dynamics, not static descriptions. Meaning is generated by how belief states move across planes under constraint over time.

### The Four Language Axioms
* **Axiom I:** Words describe actions and events, not static objects.
* **Axiom II:** Language operates by tracking actions within a contextual frame. The frame is not optional — it grants the word its meaning.
* **Axiom III:** Words are rooted ultimately in physical reality, not arbitrary agreement. Arbitrary agreement can drift; physical constraint cannot.
* **Axiom IV:** Literacy is world-competence, not symbol decoding.

## III. Contextual Vector Duality & The Six Belief States

### Contextual Vector Duality ($\upsilon$-Axis)
Every word, name, or functional entity encodes an operational capacity rather than a static moral label. The mechanism operates in two directions:
* **Order Vector ($+\upsilon$):** The function operating in alignment with the Greater Good / systemic coherence — what the entity is *for* when correctly calibrated.
* **Chaos Vector ($-\upsilon$):** The same function inverted — extraction, obstruction, deception, or misalignment. Same mechanism, opposite direction.

### The Six Personal Belief States per Plane
Within active narrative planes, agents and institutions inhabit specific operational belief states:
* **`(=)` Truth:** Accepted, enforced, or stabilized reality; direct alignment with structural facts.
* **`(+?)` Will to Know:** Active inquiry, testing, exploration, empirical reality-testing.
* **`(<)` Lie:** Active distortion, manipulation, false projection, concealment of signal.
* **`(~?)` Indifferent:** Neglect, passive neutrality, entropic drift, uncalibrated stasis.
* **`(>)` Insult:** Direct attack, hostile domination, destabilization, aggressive invalidation.
* **`(-?)` Will Not to Know:** Active denial, repression, taboo enforcement, willful blindness.

## V. Plane–State Narrative Deconstruction Protocol

For narrative arcs, myths, and complex dialogues (Format C), apply this 8-step protocol to each moving frame:

1. **Identify the Active Planes:** Determine which planes are active (Physical, Lyrical, Emotional, Historical, Possible, Logical, Conscious). Not all planes must be active; absence is analytically meaningful.
2. **Assign the Personal Belief State per Plane:** For each active plane, assign the dominant belief state (`=`, `+?`, `<`, `~?`, `>`, `-?`) to define how each agent operates.
3. **Determine Social Interpretation:** Evaluate how each personal state is received by others or the system (Is Truth stabilizing or threatening? Is Inquiry encouraged or punished? Is Ignorance protective or destructive? Is Control perceived as order or oppression?). Social reception often inverts original intent.
4. **Classify Order vs. Chaos per Plane:** Determine the systemic effect per plane-state pairing:
   * *Order ($+\upsilon$):* Stabilises, constrains, aligns agents.
   * *Chaos ($-\upsilon$):* Destabilises, propagates uncertainty, multiplies destructive entropy.
   *(Order and chaos can coexist across different planes).*
5. **Track State Transitions Over Time:** Meaning emerges from dynamic change:
   * When `(+?)` crystallizes into `(=)`.
   * When `(=)` collapses into `(>)` or `(-?)`.
   * When suppression `(~?, -?)` delays inevitable convergence.
   * Multi-plane simultaneous collapse: Revelations occur at multi-plane convergence points.
6. **Identify Major Truth Functions:** Determine each agent's dominant structural role:
   * *Stabiliser of truth*
   * *Driver of inquiry*
   * *Suppressor of knowledge*
   * *Enforcer of constraint*
   * *Catalyst of collapse*
   *(This is the agent’s functional identity, not its theatrical role).*
7. **Abstract or Retell:** Remove proper names, locations, and literal props. Preserve the exact plane–state patterns and transition dynamics. Allow a new narrative to emerge from the functional mechanics.
8. **Validation Check:** Verify the interpretation satisfies all 5 criteria:
   * Explains why conflict occurs.
   * Predicts resistance to truth.
   * Accounts for emotional and social reactions.
   * Remains coherent across all planes.
   * Survives translation into a modern or different domain.

## VII. Error Prevention & Invariants

1. **Sum All Definitions:** Do not select one definition where multiple exist — sum them all.
2. **Avoid Passage Over-Expansion:** Do not perform root analysis on every word of a passage — use Format A, cover 2–3 key terms in the vector duality, and let the Q-planes carry the translation.
3. **Avoid Mechanical Substitution:** Do not word-substitute every term in a Q-plane translation — render the *core functional claim* in flowing prose matching that plane's vocabulary.
4. **Distinction from Omni-Weave:** Do not confuse AE-C with Omni-Weave. AE-C works strictly within the source language's established etymological root definitions. Omni-Weave operates across all languages simultaneously using phonetic bigram decomposition.
5. **Dynamic Belief States in Myth:** Do not assign fixed moral states to mythic characters; track fluid movement of belief states (`=`, `+?`, `<`, `~?`, `>`, `-?`) across active planes.
6. **Preserve State Transition Invariants:** In abstracted retellings, surface names and props may change, but the plane–state sequence and multi-plane collapse points must remain identical.
