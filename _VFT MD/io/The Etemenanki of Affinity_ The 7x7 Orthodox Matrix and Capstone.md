# **The Etemenanki of Affinity: The 7x7 Orthodox Matrix and Capstone**

This framework maps the orthodox Greek concepts of love, desire, and connection. It begins with the singular totality of love, breaks it across the fundamental dimensions of human experience to form the 7 foundational base loves, and then maps the resulting 49-part human reality using the seven interrogative planes (Who, What, Where, Why, How, Cause, Effect).

## **The Base Division: The Foundational Mapping**

To form the 7 base loves, the absolute totality of love fractures downward across the dimensions of life, anchoring the primary origins of human affection directly to their governing planes of reality.

### **THE DRIVER (The Emergent Origin)**

* **Q1: The Plane of Identity (WHO) – Will and Direction.**  
  * **Agape (ἀγάπη) \[ah-GAH-peh\].** Unconditional Love.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* The highest form of selfless, universal, and unconditioned affection; seeking the ultimate objective good of another without expectation of return.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The absolute origin point of love. It is a pure, self-sustaining outpouring of devotion that requires no external prompt, no equal exchange, and no prerequisite of beauty or usefulness. It drives the entire system of love from the top down by giving simply to give.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "You owe me nothing, and you have nothing I need, but my will is to ensure your absolute highest good."

### **THE VERTICAL AXIS (The Soul: Origin and Consequence)**

Here, Agape fractures into two halves: the foundation of the self and the connection to the other.

* **Q6: The Historical Plane (CAUSE) – The Origin of the Self.**  
  * **Philautia (φιλαυτία) \[fil-aw-TEE-ah\].** Self-Love (The Foundation).  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Healthy self-regard and self-compassion; the foundational requirement to care for oneself before caring for others.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The necessary starting point. It is the active care and stabilization of one's own foundation, establishing personal health and strength so that the individual has the actual capacity to pour love out to others without collapsing.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "I must secure my own foundation and health before I have the strength to support anyone else."  
* **Q7: The Emotive Plane (EFFECT) – The Consequence of Connection.**  
  * **Philia (φιλία) \[fi-LEE-ah\].** Brotherly Love (The Shared Bond).  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Deep, affectionate friendship and loyalty based on mutual respect, shared values, and equality.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The beautiful consequence of connection. It is the perfectly balanced, mutual exchange of intellect, respect, and devotion between equals, resulting in profound harmony and a shared passion for life.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "We are absolute equals, sharing total respect and moral alignment as a unified bond."

### **THE LATERAL AXIS (The Body: Needs and Space)**

The self-aligned branch fractures into the physical drives of the body, seeking to fill emptiness and cross physical space.

* **Q2: The Possible Plane (WHAT) – The Drive to Satisfy.**  
  * **Epithymia (ἐπιθυμία) \[ep-ee-thoo-MEE-ah\].** Appetitive Desire.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Strong desire, lust, or the base physical appetite seeking bodily satisfaction.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The raw, physical appetite reaching out toward an object to consume it. It is driven entirely by the need to satisfy a deep internal emptiness or physical craving.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "I am physically empty, and I must consume that object to satisfy my base craving."  
* **Q3: The Physical Plane (WHERE) – The Drive to Merge.**  
  * **Eros (ἔρως) \[AIR-os\].** Passionate Love.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Intense physical attraction, desire, and the passionate romance that seeks union.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The volatile, overwhelming drive to destroy the physical distance between two people, seeking absolute union and the merging of two bodies and spirits into one.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "I am entirely consumed by the drive to close the distance and merge my physical space with yours."

### **THE LONGITUDINAL AXIS (The Mind: Meaning and Duty)**

The other-aligned branch fractures into the relational drives of the mind, defining our duties and how we expand our hearts.

* **Q4: The Lyrical Plane (WHY) – Meaning and Expansion.**  
  * **Philoxenia (φιλοξενία) \[fil-ox-en-EE-ah\].** Hospitality.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* The virtuous love of strangers; welcoming, sheltering, and caring for the traveler.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The highest conscious choice of the mind to find meaning by actively opening the borders of the home and the heart to welcome, protect, and embrace the unknown or foreign outsider.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "Though you are entirely foreign to me, my door is open and my table is yours."  
* **Q5: The Logical Plane (HOW) – Rhythm and Duty.**  
  * **Storge (στοργή) \[stor-GAY\].** Familial Love.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* The natural, instinctual affection and loyalty born of familiarity, kinship, and blood.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The deep-seated natural rhythm and undeniable instinct that binds a family together. It operates as a constant, underlying pull that ensures the loyalty, protection, and survival of one's own flesh and blood.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "Blood is thicker than water; my protection of you is a deep, natural instinct and is absolute."

## **Q1 Plane: WHO (The Plane of Identity and Will)**

This plane governs absolute identity, core purpose, and the highest spiritual unifications of the self to the divine and to humanity.

* **Q1q1 (Who of Who): Thelema (θέλημα) \[thel-AY-mah\].** Core Volition.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* The sovereign will, active intention, or underlying wish of a conscious subject.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The fundamental engine of the human heart; the unshakeable, underlying purpose originating from the absolute center of a person that actively drives all their choices.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "This is my sovereign choice and my deepest purpose, regardless of the resistance I face."  
* **Q1q2 (What of Who): Philanthropia (φιλανθρωπία) \[fil-an-thro-PEE-ah\].** Love of Humanity.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Benevolence, charity, and broad affection for all human beings.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The broad embrace of human identity; the charitable affection sparked by the innate recognition that we share the exact same human nature, leading one to pour care out to the collective.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "We share the exact same human nature; therefore, your inherent dignity is my own."  
* **Q1q3 (Where of Who): Koinonia (κοινωνία) \[koy-no-NEE-ah\].** Communion / Fellowship.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Deep spiritual sharing, fellowship, and joint participation.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The shared home of identity; a profound spiritual integration that binds multiple individuals into a deeply unified, synchronized community of believers or partners.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "We are living, breathing members of the exact same spiritual family, functioning as one."  
* **Q1q4 (Why of Who): Philokalia (φιλοκαλία) \[fil-oh-kah-LEE-ah\].** Love of the Beautiful/Good.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* A highly refined, spiritual affinity for what is aesthetically, morally, and transcendently excellent.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The highest pursuit of the soul; a pure, upward reaching of the heart where a person anchors their identity directly to the most perfect, objective truth and moral beauty available.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "My soul is drawn relentlessly upward toward the highest, most perfect truth and beauty."  
* **Q1q5 (How of Who): Threskeia (θρησκεία) \[thres-KAY-ah\].** Religious Devotion.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Religious worship, outward liturgy, and strict ritual observance.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The procedural rhythm of belief; the active, repeated, and physical practice of traditions and rituals that keep a person's identity firmly anchored to the divine.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "I maintain this strict daily ritual to ensure my heart remains tethered to its maker."  
* **Q1q6 (Cause of Who): Eusebeia (εὐσέβεια) \[yoo-seb-AY-ah\].** Piety.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Reverence for the divine, respect for the natural order, and the dutiful fulfillment of sacred obligations.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The humble submission of the self; a state of deep reverence where a person voluntarily bows their own ego and shapes their life to honor the overarching laws of God or nature.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "I willingly bow my own pride out of profound reverence for the ultimate Creator."  
* **Q1q7 (Effect of Who): Henosis (ἕνωσις) \[HEN-oh-sis\].** Mystical Union.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Mystical oneness, absolute union, or singularity with the divine reality.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The ultimate peace of identity; the terminal, transcendent state where the boundaries separating the individual from God are completely dissolved, bringing the soul into perfect, infinite rest.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "The boundary separating my individual self from the infinite divine has completely dissolved into perfect peace."

## **Q2 Plane: WHAT (The Plane of Targets and Desires)**

This plane (anchored by *Epithymia*) governs love directed toward concepts, physical targets, ambition, and the raw drives to acquire and consume.

* **Q2q1 (Who of What): Philotimia (φιλοτιμία) \[fil-oh-tee-MEE-ah\].** Love of Honor.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Ambition, the drive for civic distinction, reputation, and respect among peers.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The drive to establish one's name; the active pursuit of greatness to expand one's reputation, build a legacy, and secure verified respect in the eyes of the public.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "I will execute this great work to earn the absolute respect and recognition of my peers."  
* **Q2q2 (What of What): Epithymia (ἐπιθυμία) \[ep-ee-thoo-MEE-ah\].** Appetitive Desire.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Strong desire, lust, or base physical appetite seeking bodily satisfaction.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The raw, unrefined physical urge of the body; a consuming drive focused entirely on acquiring a target to satisfy a deep, internal physical craving.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "I am physically empty, and I must consume that object to satisfy my base craving."  
* **Q2q3 (Where of What): Philarguria (φιλαργυρία) \[fil-ar-goo-REE-ah\].** Love of Money.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Greed, avarice, and the love of silver or wealth.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The corrupted love of the material; a dark, hoarding obsession focused entirely on accumulating dead material wealth as a false substitute for true spiritual or emotional security.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "I will aggressively hoard this dead material wealth to simulate power and security."  
* **Q2q4 (Why of What): Zelos (ζῆλος) \[ZAY-los\].** Zeal / Emulation.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Fervent dedication or the productive desire to emulate and equal the greatness of another.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The inspiration of greatness; the productive, fiery desire sparked by seeing someone else's excellence, which fuels a person to grow and elevate their own life to match it without wishing the other harm.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "I observe your supreme excellence, and it fuels my drive to grow and elevate my own life to match it."  
* **Q2q5 (How of What): Philomatheia (φιλομάθεια) \[fil-oh-mah-THAY-ah\].** Love of Learning.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* A deep, driving affinity for acquiring knowledge, wisdom, and understanding.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The pursuit of truth; the active, relentless gathering of external facts and data to expand the mind's understanding and clarity of the world.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "I must relentlessly acquire this hidden wisdom to expand my understanding of reality."  
* **Q2q6 (Cause of What): Orexis (ὄρεξις) \[or-EX-is\].** Appetite / Striving.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* The innate striving, reaching out, or natural appetite for fulfillment.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The deep instinct of need; the natural, innate reaching of the soul as it stretches out toward anything it recognizes as necessary for its survival or fulfillment.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "My soul inherently and naturally stretches out toward that which it recognizes as necessary."  
* **Q2q7 (Effect of What): Lagneia (λαγνεία) \[lag-NAY-ah\].** Lechery / Lust.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Unrestrained, corrupted sexual lust and lasciviousness lacking modesty.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The dark terminal point of desire; a violently selfish urge that violently takes from another without limits, ending only when the target is entirely used up and broken.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "I will selfishly use you up entirely and without stopping to satisfy my raw lust."

## **Q3 Plane: WHERE (The Plane of Boundaries and Distance)**

This plane (anchored by *Eros*) governs love defined by physical closeness, the crossing of boundaries, total union, and the ache of distance.

* **Q3q1 (Who of Where): Synodos (σύνοδος) \[SYN-od-os\].** Traveling Together.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* A traveling together, assembly, conjunction, or moving in a shared direction.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The companionship of the road; two or more people walking the exact same path through life, maintaining continuous closeness and shared direction without entirely losing their individual identities.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "We are moving side-by-side along the exact same path through life."  
* **Q3q2 (What of Where): Sygkrasis (σύγκρασις) \[syng-KRAH-sis\].** Commingling.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* The intimate mixing, blending, or tempering together of distinct temperaments.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The profound blending of souls; a rare state where distinct personalities perfectly complement and pour into one another, sharing the exact same emotional space without destroying who they individually are.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "Our lives have blended so perfectly that we now occupy the exact same emotional space."  
* **Q3q3 (Where of Where): Eros (ἔρως) \[AIR-os\].** Passionate Love.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Intense physical attraction, desire, and passionate romance.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The absolute craving for union; the volatile, high-velocity drive attempting to destroy all physical and spiritual distance between two lovers, seeking to become one.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "I am entirely consumed by the drive to close the distance and merge my body and spirit with yours."  
* **Q3q4 (Why of Where): Pothos (πόθος) \[POTH-os\].** Longing for the Absent.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Yearning or deep longing for someone or something that is distant or lost.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The weight of absence; the aching, hollow space left behind in the heart when a deeply loved person is ripped away, continuously drawing tears and energy inward.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "The physical space you left behind is a crushing, hollow ache that continuously draws my tears."  
* **Q3q5 (How of Where): Himeros (ἵμερος) \[HIM-er-os\].** Acute Yearning.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Immediate, pressing, and unfulfilled physical desire or longing.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The friction of proximity; the intense tension, heat, and pressing desire generated when two lovers are incredibly close to one another but have not yet touched or merged.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "Your immediate physical closeness generates an intense, pressing heat I can barely contain."  
* **Q3q6 (Cause of Where): Sevas (σέβας) \[SEB-as\].** Reverential Fear.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Awe, profound respect bordering on dread, experienced before majesty.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The boundary of awe; an encounter with something or someone whose presence is so overwhelmingly majestic and powerful that it forces a person to freeze in their tracks out of sheer reverence.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "Your majesty is so overwhelming that I must halt my approach and bow in awe."  
* **Q3q7 (Effect of Where): Aidos (αἰδώς) \[eye-DOS\].** Modesty / Restraint.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Constructive shame, reverence, modesty, and deep respect for moral boundaries.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The honor of distance; the beautiful internal restraint and reverence that recognizes and deeply respects the personal boundaries of another, refusing to forcefully trespass upon them.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "I acutely respect the sacred perimeter of your boundaries and will not trespass upon them."

## **Q4 Plane: WHY (The Plane of Meaning and Compassion)**

This plane (anchored by *Philoxenia*) governs love defined by deep compassion, the welcoming of strangers, the pouring out of mercy, and the pursuit of meaning.

* **Q4q1 (Who of Why): Eunoia (εὔνοια) \[yoo-NOY-ah\].** Goodwill / Beautiful Thinking.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* A favorable disposition, pure intent, and active well-wishing toward another.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The foundational posture of the heart; a pure, quiet state of mind where a person actively hopes for the absolute best for someone else, holding no malice or hidden agenda.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "My thoughts toward you are inherently beautiful, and my heart actively hopes for your good."  
* **Q4q2 (What of Why): Philotechnia (φιλοτεχνία) \[fil-oh-tek-NEE-ah\].** Love of Art / Craft.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Devotion to skill, craftsmanship, and the creation of beautiful or useful things.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The pouring of the soul into matter; the active devotion of taking an internal vision and carefully shaping raw, chaotic material into deliberate beauty and order.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "I will carefully shape this raw material until it perfectly mirrors the beauty in my mind."  
* **Q4q3 (Where of Why): Oikeiōsis (οἰκείωσις) \[oy-kay-OH-sis\].** Appropriation.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* The Stoic concept of familiarization, making one's own, and expanding the sphere of concern.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The expansion of the family; the profound ethical choice to look at an outsider, a stranger, or the world itself, and pull them into the protective embrace of one's own family and personal care.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "I have successfully expanded the borders of my heart to care for you as part of my own family."  
* **Q4q4 (Why of Why): Philoxenia (φιλοξενία) \[fil-ox-en-EE-ah\].** Hospitality.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* The virtuous practice of welcoming, sheltering, and caring for guests or strangers.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The welcoming of the unknown; the deliberate suspension of fear toward outsiders, opening the doors of one's home to offer warmth, food, and safety to a traveler.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "Though you are entirely foreign to me, my door is open and my table is yours."  
* **Q4q5 (How of Why): Xenia (ξενία) \[ksen-EE-ah\].** Guest-Friendship.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* A formalized, sacred bond of hospitality ensuring mutual protection across borders.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The unbreakable bridge of peace; a sacred, formalized promise between two different families or tribes that guarantees mutual shelter, respect, and defense across generations.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "Our families are bound by an unbreakable, inter-generational promise of mutual shelter and defense."  
* **Q4q6 (Cause of Why): Splagchnon (σπλάγχνον) \[SPLAHNGK-non\].** Visceral Compassion.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* The deepest, gut-level compassion and mercy felt at the core of one's being.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The physical ache of empathy; the very core of a person vibrating in deep, uncontrollable sympathy when witnessing another's profound suffering, bypassing all logic to force an immediate desire to help.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "Your acute suffering physically aches in the absolute center of my being."  
* **Q4q7 (Effect of Why): Eleos (ἔλεος) \[EL-eh-os\].** Compassion / Mercy.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Deep pity, mercy, and active compassion shown to those who are afflicted.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The reaching hand of mercy; care and aid actively poured downward to lift up, protect, and heal someone who is suffering, broken, or drowning in the hardships of life.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "You are drowning in the depths, and I am reaching my hand downward to pull you from the water."

## **Q5 Plane: HOW (The Plane of Rhythm and Duty)**

This plane (anchored by *Storge*) governs love defined by loyalty, natural family bonds, practical endurance, and standing shoulder-to-shoulder in unity.

  * *Orthodox Definition:* Concord, solidarity, shared purpose, and a community thinking as one.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The harmony of purpose; a beautiful state of peace where a community completely sets aside its differences to share the exact same understanding and walk together toward a single goal.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "We have set aside our division to share one mind and one heart toward our goal."  
* **Q5q3 (Where of How): Kedeia (κηδεία) \[kay-DAY-ah\].** Devotion to the Dead.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Profound care, funerary rites, and ongoing loyalty to those who have passed away.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The love that outlives life; profound loyalty and devotion carried across the boundary of death, ensuring those who have passed are honored, remembered, and kept alive in the family's heart.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "Though your breath has ceased, my sacred duty to honor your memory will never end."  
* **Q5q4 (Why of How): Philostorgia (φιλοστοργία) \[fil-oh-stor-GEE-ah\].** Tender Affection.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* A warm, devoted affection combining natural familial bonding with chosen friendship.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The perfection of family love; the beautiful merging of natural, inescapable family duty with the joyous, deliberate warmth of choosing your kin as your best friends.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "You are not just my family by duty; you are the friend I actively and warmly choose."  
* **Q5q5 (How of How): Storge (στοργή) \[stor-GAY\].** Familial Love.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* The natural, instinctual affection and loyalty born out of familiarity and kinship.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The bedrock of the home; the natural, undeniable pull and instinctual devotion that a mother feels for a child, securing the survival, protection, and warmth of the family.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "Blood is thicker than water; my instinct to protect you is deep and absolute."  
* **Q5q6 (Cause of How): Astorgos (ἄστοργος) \[ah-STOR-gos\].** Heartlessness.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* The tragic or unnatural absence of natural familial love and basic affection for kin.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The breaking of the bloodline; the cold, tragic void where natural affection fails to exist, collapsing the basic trust of the family and leaving deep, unnatural wounds.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "You have abandoned the basic love owed to your own blood; you are dead to me."  
* **Q5q7 (Effect of How): Pragma (πρᾶγμα) \[PRAHG-mah\].** Practical / Enduring Love.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Mature, longstanding love built on duty, patience, compromise, and shared life.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The settling of the foundation; the beautiful transition of fiery, temporary passion into permanent, weight-bearing devotion that withstands the trials of decades through patience and shared labor.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "The initial fire may have faded, but I will carry the heavy, beautiful weight of this life together."

  * *Example Phrase:* "I am being violently overwhelmed by this emotion, suffering its weight completely."

This plane (anchored by *Philia*) governs love defined by perfect harmony, playful joy, long-suffering patience, and the flawless return of affection.

* **Q7q1 (Who of Effect): Charis (χάρις) \[KHAH-ris\].** Grace / Gratitude.

* **Q7q2 (What of Effect): Paidia (παιδιά) \[py-DEE-ah\] / Ludus.** Playful Love.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Flirtatious, uncommitted, game-like interaction focusing on the sport of the moment.  
  * *Action-Effect Mechanics:* The joy of the moment; a light, playful exchange of attention and laughter that delights in the present without demanding any heavy promises or long-term consequences.  
  * *Example Phrase:* "We are dancing in a light, joyful game, entirely free from the heavy burden of consequence."  
* **Q7q3 (Where of Effect): Symphonia (συμφωνία) \[sym-fo-NEE-ah\].** Harmony.  
  * *Orthodox Definition:* Agreement of minds, harmonious relations, and perfect unison.
