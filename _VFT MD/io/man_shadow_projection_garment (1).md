Man as Shadow, Projection, and Garment


## "Who told you that you were naked?"

You're wearing the body.

The mind is separate from and uses the soul to process information from the body, which picks up from the universe through its several senses. You are in God (John 14). The universe is the outermost layer you inhabit.

Man is the reflection-projection of God. Not a copy. The shape of what God defined for himself. God can take any form, which means the only entity capable of defining God's form is God. He is the original definer of Truth. Any external definition requires a reference frame outside the totality, which doesn't exist.

God made man in his shadow. Not his image. The Hebrew tselem shares its root with tzel — shadow, shade. A shadow cast into physical reality is an impression. The source pressed into the material.

The shadow is not a passive reflection. It is a projection with force. The light is thrown into the surface. Creation is not where God placed himself. It is where God aimed.

This is why Jesus entering his own creation as Man was necessary. You cannot transmit accurate information about the source through a vessel that will reject it before it lands. The definer had to enter the system directly, man to man, from inside the same vessel type, with no translation layer. 1 Cor 4:6 carries specific weight for exactly this reason.


## Wineskins, Garments, and the Body

The body is the most direct rebuke against dogma and orthodox corruption available. It is the primary vessel. Not a metaphor for one.

Wineskins and garments teach the same mechanic: containers hold knowledge, and old containers cannot hold new knowledge without structural failure. New wine fermenting in an old wineskin generates pressure the hardened structure cannot flex to accommodate. It bursts. You lose both.

The garment parable runs on identical principles to organ rejection. You cut a patch from a new garment to cover a tear in an old one. This destroys the integrity of the new garment and the old system still rejects the patch because the material is incompatible with its existing tension and structure. In biology this is the full inflammatory cascade: the host mounts a systemic defense and the resulting necrosis can kill the host entirely. The attempt to preserve the old system by grafting new thinking onto it produces a worse outcome than the original tear.

The only coherent move is a new wineskin. A new perspective built from the ground up without the assumption load of the previous container. Any prior assumption about the subject degrades the quality of the new reading.
