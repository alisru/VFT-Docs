# The Heart Sutra (Prajnaparamita Hrdaya)

## The Non-Duality of Emptiness and Form

Thus have I heard: Once the Blessed One was dwelling in Rajagriha at Vulture Peak mountain, together with a great assembly of monks and bodhisattvas. At that time the Blessed One was absorbed in the samadhi called Profundity of Illumination.

At the same time, the noble Avalokiteshvara, the Bodhisattva Mahasattva, was contemplating the profound Prajnaparamita, and he saw that the five aggregates (skandhas) were empty of inherent nature.

Then, by the power of the Buddha, the venerable Shariputra addressed the noble Avalokiteshvara: How should a noble son or noble daughter train who wishes to practice the profound Prajnaparamita?

The noble Avalokiteshvara replied:

Form is emptiness, emptiness is form. Emptiness is not other than form, form is not other than emptiness. In the same way, feeling, perception, mental formations, and consciousness are emptiness.

Thus, Shariputra, all phenomena are characterized by emptiness: they are without birth, without cessation, without stain, without purity, without decrease, and without increase.

Therefore, Shariputra, in emptiness there is no form, no feeling, no perception, no mental formation, no consciousness; no eye, no ear, no nose, no tongue, no body, no mind; no sight, no sound, no smell, no taste, no touch, no mental object; no ignorance, no end of ignorance, up to no aging and death; no suffering, no origin of suffering, no cessation of suffering, and no path; no wisdom, no attainment, and no non-attainment.

Gate Gate Paragate Parasamgate Bodhi Svaha!
