$$\mathcal{V}_{\text{conserv}} = \max\left(0, 1 - \frac{\Delta \mathcal{V}}{1.5 \cdot (\mathcal{F}_{\text{expected}} + \mathcal{B}_{\text{expected}} + \epsilon)}\right)$$
