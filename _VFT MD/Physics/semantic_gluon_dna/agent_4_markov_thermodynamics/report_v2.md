## 5. Critique & Architectural Upgrades for `unified_gluon_dna_engine.py`

Based on empirical validation across all 26 documents (`chat`, `irm_summary`, 15 `irm_formal_corpus` papers, and 9 `arxiv_samples` papers), here are the **4 high-impact upgrades** to integrate into `unified_gluon_dna_engine.py`:

1. **Decouple `P -> P -> P` into *Substantiated Proof Chains* vs. *Unverified Speculative Runaways***:
   - In `unified_gluon_dna_engine.py` v1, any `P -> P -> P` sequence was penalized as a "Productive Runaway". However, in `arxiv_samples` (and the IRM Formal Papers), multi-step mathematical derivations (`Lemma -> Substitution -> Grönwall Inequality -> Theorem`) naturally produce `P -> P -> P -> R -> P` chains! By checking `is_working_step` (presence of relational bounds `\leq, \geq, =`, proof operators, or benchmark verification), we separate **rigorous proof chains** (rewarded by the *Working-Out Rigor Index*) from **unverified narrative assertions**.
2. **Add the *Working-Out Rigor Index* ($W_{\text{rigor}} \in [0, 100]$) to the Unified Maturity Formula**:
   - Pure Markov stationary similarity (`1 - JSD`) can be fooled by short axiomatic summaries that match surface modal counts without doing any mathematical work. Combining `0.65 * Markov_Maturity + 0.35 * Working_Out_Rigor_Alignment` cleanly separates axiomatic executive summaries (`Comprehensive Report - IRM.md`: Rigor `58.05`, Pub Maturity `62.60`) from full working formal papers (IRM Formal Corpus Avg: Rigor `64.89`, Pub Maturity `69.21`; Top Formal Paper `Formal_Paper_9__Higher-Order_Simplicial_Homology__Principal_G-Bundles_.md`: Rigor `76.02`, Pub Maturity `82.45`).
3. **Multi-Document Ecosystem Concept Survival (Summary vs. Formal Corpus Matrix)**:
   - Upgrade single-pair concept survival (`chat -> irm`) to an $N$-document survival matrix. Our Round 2 engine proves that **cross-document concept survival increases from `32.9%` (in `Comprehensive Report` alone) to `37.6%` across the full `irm_formal_corpus`**, automatically pinpointing which formal paper rescues each concept (`4` rescued) and which `13` high-fitness concepts (`mixed-radix digits`, `Rod Numerals`, `Pre-Reality Math`) still need a dedicated formal bridge note.
4. **Stationary-Weighted $8\times 8$ Modal JSD alongside $4\times 4$ Engine JSD**:
   - Within each Engine quadrant (`C = is_like/may_be`, `P = will_be/can_be`, `R = is_not/cant_be`, `G = was_like/wont_be`), the ratio of the two sub-modals matters enormously: e.g., in `P`, `can_be` (bounded inequality / empirical test) vs `will_be` (deductive implication) distinguishes PDE analysis (`Navier-Stokes`) from pure algebraic rewrites. Including the $8\times 8$ modal transition divergence provides finer diagnostic resolution.
