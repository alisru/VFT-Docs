## Step 3: Cross-Tradition Native Scriptural Re-Projections

### A. The Vedic / Bhagavad Gita Re-Projection
*(Context: The Field of Dharma, Kshetra and Kshetrajna, avoiding Varna-Sankara, and Yajna)*

> The Blessed Lord said:
> "Hear now, O Partha, the true distinction between the Field (*Kshetra*) and the Knower of the Field (*Kshetrajna*). When a hostile clan pollutes the sacred boundary and introduces confusion into the cosmic order, the warrior who is established in yoga must not yield to weakness of heart.
>
> The men of the opposing clan are initiating seeds of adharma; if their seed-bearers and their young remain, the cycle of conflict will sprout anew. And the women who have known the men of that clan are fields already imprinted with foreign karma; to bring them into your borders is to cause *varna-sankara*—the catastrophic mixing of lineages that corrupts the ancestral rites and drags the clan into ruin. Therefore, let the sword of dispassionate duty dissolve the tainted fields and the rival seeds.
>
> But the maidens who are unseeded—they are pure, uncarved Prakriti. They carry no memory of the fallen kings. Receive them into your borders, not for lust, but as unblemished fields to be consecrated under Dharma. And when you return from the field of battle, bring the gold and the cattle to the sacrificial fire (*Agni*). For that which endures fire is cleansed of blood-karma, and that which is soft must be washed in holy water. Offer the first portion to the Brahmins who tend the altar, divide the remainder justly between those who fought and those who sustained the camp, and harbor no personal pride in the victory."

### B. The Taoist (Daodejing) Re-Projection
*(Context: The Valley Spirit, The Uncarved Block, and Warfare as a Funeral Rite)*

> The Valley Spirit never dies; it is called the Mysterious Female. The gateway of the Mysterious Female is the root of Heaven and Earth.
>
> When the kingdom forgets simplicity, artificial desires entangle the borders. The people of Midian opened the gates of excess, and a great sickness swallowed twenty-four thousand. To restore the balance, the ruler must cut away the entanglement, but he must observe the limits of Heaven.
>
> Those who project aggressive force (*Yang*) and have set their minds upon rivalry must perish, for that which is rigid breaks before the Tao. The women who have received their seed are like wood already carved into fixed shapes—they can never return to the Uncarved Block (*Pu*). But the young daughters who have known no man are still the raw, uncarved wood; their valley is empty, receptive, and untainted by contrivance.
>
> Yet let the general remember Chapter 31: *Weapons are instruments of ill omen, not the tools of the wise.* Even in complete triumph, there is no cause for rejoicing. He who rejoices in victory rejoices in slaughter. Therefore, the victorious soldiers must dwell outside the walls for seven days, observing the rites of mourning. The metals must pass through fire, and the garments through water, to strip away the stench of violence before entering the peaceful dwelling. The spoils must be dispersed to all corners, for Heaven diminishes the excessive to feed the modest.

### C. The Buddhist Sutra Re-Projection
*(Context: Dependent Origination, Karmic Seeds, and the Poison of Grasping)*

> Thus have I heard:
> The Blessed One was dwelling in the Jeta Grove, and the monks questioned him concerning the ancient wars of the desert kings. The Blessed One said:
>
> "Look upon this, O Bhikkhus: through ignorance (*avijjā*), craving arose; through craving, grasping (*upādāna*); and through grasping, mutual destruction. The people of Midian and the sons of the desert clung to their tribal identities as separate selves (*attā*). Mingling in lust and unwholesome rites at Peor, the friction of their contact brought forth the pestilence.
>
> Then Moses, bound by the necessity of preserving his clan's vessel, commanded the sword. He saw that the minds of the adult men and the mothers were deeply impregnated with defiled impressions (*saṅkhāras*) and toxic karmic seeds (*bījas*). Fearing that these seeds would ripen into future suffering within his own camp, he ordered them extinguished. But the young maidens, having formed no such karmic bonds, were spared like clean soil.
>
> Yet consider the suffering, Bhikkhus! The soldiers washed in water and burned their weapons in fire, believing that fire can cleanse blood. But though fire melts gold, it cannot burn away the volition of slaughter (*cetanā*). The commanders trembled in their tents because, having taken thousands of lives while losing none of their own, the weight of their unripened karma pressed upon their hearts. Thus they brought gifts of gold to the altar, seeking peace of mind. Truly I say to you: hatred does not cease by hatred; only by letting go of the boundary between 'ours' and 'theirs' is the wheel of becoming brought to rest."

### D. The Islamic / Tawhidic Re-Projection
*(Context: Fitrah, Elimination of Fasad, Law of Ghanima, and Taharah)*

> In the Name of God, the Merciful, the Compassionate.
>
> When those who break their covenants spread corruption in the land (*fasad fil-ard*) and lure the believers toward idolatry (*shirk*), God commands that the hand of justice be raised against them. Thus the children of Israel were ordered to strike the leaders of Midian, who had plotted to bring down the wrath of heaven upon the righteous.
>
> The warriors slew the tyrants and took captive their households. But Moses rebuked them, saying: 'Will you preserve those who seduced the faithful and caused the plague?' For the adult women who participated in the treachery had corrupted their original nature. But the maiden who has known no man remains in her primordial purity (*Fitrah*)—her heart has not been sealed with the idolatry of her fathers, and she may be brought into the fold of faith.
>
> And when the victory was won, the spoils of war (*Ghanima*) were not left to greed or dispute. As God ordained: a portion belongs to the sanctuary, to the priests, and to the Levites who serve the house of God, while the remainder is divided in equal measure between the warriors who bore the heat of battle and the community that sustained them. And before any man who took life or touched the slain may enter the camp of the believers, he must observe the laws of ritual purity (*Taharah*), washing himself and passing all that he took through fire and water. For God loves those who turn to Him in repentance and those who purify themselves.
