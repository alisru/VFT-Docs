Senator HANSON (Queensland—Leader of Pauline Hanson's One Nation) (18:17): With the exception of the need to support extended deadlines and the greater transparency and compliance measures in the water market, you can bet the farm that One Nation will not be supporting this bill. The Water Amendment (Restoring Our Rivers) Bill 2023 is gambling with the farms in a region that produces more than $22 billion worth of Australia's food and fibre, gambling with the fate of this production in the Murray-Darling Basin while stacking the odds against it. I shouldn't have to remind the Senate that the Murray-Darling Basin Plan is supposed to be a balanced scheme supporting better environmental outcomes in addition to ensuring sustainable irrigation industries and regional economies. The balance is thrown out with this bill.

[Full speech continues for approx. 1000 more words opposing water buybacks, foreign ownership of water, and Indigenous cultural water provisions; confirmed via Hanson's Senate vote of "No" on the bill, 30 November 2023, They Vote For You.]
