TITLE: 'Lies': Hanson urges Aussies to ignore Welcome to Country ceremonies in wake of AFL controversy
PUBLISHED: 2024-09-18
PRIMARY URL: https://www.onenation.org.au/lies-hanson-urges-aussies-to-ignore-welcome-to-country
MIRROR URL: https://qld.onenation.org.au/lies-hanson-urges-aussies-to-ignore-welcome-to-country

---CONTENT (excerpt)---

One Nation leader Pauline Hanson has vowed to 'turn her back' on Welcome to Country ceremonies and urged "fed up" Australians to join her.

The One Nation leader delivered a fiery statement in the Senate on Tuesday afternoon, describing the tradition as "divisive" and something "many people tell me they are just over".

Her remarks follow ongoing controversy over a Welcome to Country performed before the GWS Giants and Brisbane Lions AFL semi-final clash on Saturday (14 September 2024) at ENGIE Stadium in Sydney, where cultural educator Brendan Kerin stated the ceremony was "not for white people."

Senator Hanson said in the Senate:

"If they (Welcome to Country ceremonies) are not to cater to white people, then why are white people constantly subjected to them?"

"These welcomes are based on lies that Australia is not our home. So many people tell me they are just over it."
