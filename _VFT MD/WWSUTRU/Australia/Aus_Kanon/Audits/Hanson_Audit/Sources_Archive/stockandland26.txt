TITLE: Pauline Hanson demands total ban on foreign ownership of Australian farmland
PUBLISHED: 2026-05-29 (quoting a 24 May 2026 interview)
PRIMARY URL: https://www.stockandland.com.au/story/9256154/pauline-hanson-demands-a-total-ban-on-foreign-farmland-ownership

---CONTENT (excerpt)---

One Nation leader Pauline Hanson has called on the federal government to place a total ban on foreign ownership of Australian farmland.

Speaking to a journalist from New Zealand this week, Ms Hanson said 53 million hectares of Australia's prime agricultural land was held by foreign interests.

"This equates to 14 per cent of Australia's total 372 million hectares of agricultural land," she said.

She praised the approach the New Zealand government was taking in "protecting its valuable farmland."

"New Zealand's very smart because they've only put a 25-year lease on farming land," she said.

"I don't believe that foreigners should own any housing in Australia or our farming land."

Ms Hanson said One Nation would push the government to ban any further sale of freehold farmland to foreign investors and limit the sale of leasehold farmland to a tenure of 25 years.

"One Nation will also reimplement Foreign Investment Review Board applications on all agricultural land from expiring free trade agreements," she said.
