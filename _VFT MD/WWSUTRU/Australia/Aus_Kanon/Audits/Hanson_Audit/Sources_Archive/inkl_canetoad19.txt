TITLE: Hanson's 'terrible' cash-for-cane-toads idea won't work, experts say
PUBLISHED: 2019-01-08
PRIMARY URL: https://www.inkl.com/news/hanson-s-terrible-cash-for-cane-toads-idea-won-t-work-experts-say

---CONTENT (excerpt)---

In her pitch to the Prime Minister, Senator Hanson focuses on the eradication of adult cane toads who "eat almost anything it can fit in its mouth".

"When rabbits plagued our nation, a sizeable reward was posted for the biological control of the species," she wrote.

Senator Hanson has written to Prime Minister Scott Morrison to back a federally funded scheme in Queensland, NSW, Western Australia and the Northern Territory as a way to eradicate the cane toad, proposing a bounty payment for people who catch and kill them.
