# Kanon Audit Lessons & Rules

## 1. Anti-Overproofing in Actuality Sections
* **No Overproofing**: Keep the Actuality section focused strictly on the primary, most direct, and verifiable action(s) needed to prove the vector verdict (e.g., the Robodebt Royal Commission for the Royal Commission vector). 
* **Do Not Add Unnecessary Examples**: Do not pull in unrelated secondary examples, recent debates, or additional cases just to "overprove" the point. Adding unnecessary examples increases the risk of factual errors, timeline discrepancies, and token waste.
* **Verify Full Timelines**: If you must include a historical detail, you must verify the full timeline of the action. Do not report an initial refusal or statement as a final position if subsequent events or policy reversals occurred (e.g., reporting that Albanese resisted/rejected the Bondi Beach Royal Commission, when he actually established it just weeks later on January 8, 2026).

## 2. Hypothesis Generation Prompting
* **Strict Self-Prompting**: Before generating a hypothesis or searching for any node, you must explicitly write down and evaluate the following question:
  `"How does Albanese hit or miss [whole node JSON text] in a quote?"`
  You must include the complete, unredacted JSON object of the node (containing name, canonical_quote, description, establishes, coordinates, etc.) in this question to ensure you evaluate the actual political mechanism rather than a generic or abstract topic noun.
