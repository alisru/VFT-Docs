The Treasury Laws Amendment (Energy Price Relief Plan) Act 2022 was passed by the Australian Parliament on 15 December 2022. The Act introduced price caps on wholesale gas (capped at $12 per gigajoule) and coal prices to shield Australian households and businesses from skyrocketing electricity costs caused by global energy shocks.
