The Albanese Government announced a $45.2 million investment in the 2023-24 Budget to address a critical backlog of repair work at Sydney Harbour Federation Trust sites, preserving Federation-era historical public spaces for future generations, including Cockatoo Island/Wareamah, Headland Park, and Fitzroy Dry Dock.
