### 1.1 The Moral Axis ($\upsilon$): Beneficiary Scope
The horizontal axis $\upsilon$ answers the foundational question: **"Who does this idea benefit?"**

$$\text{Scope} = \frac{N_{\text{beneficiaries}}}{N_{\text{total\_system}}}$$

The discrete geometric anchors defined on the Hegemony are:
* **$+2.0\upsilon$ — {Everyone}:** *"Take to give to everyone."* Universal Systemic Justice, ecological custody, truth across all beings. Unbounded horizon.
* **$+1.0\upsilon$ — Other Beings:** *"Give to everyone."* The Good Truth / Greater Good. Altruistic collective action benefiting humanity beyond the self.
* **$+0.3\upsilon \text{ to } +0.5\upsilon$ — Other Being:** Targeted altruism toward a specific other individual.
* **$0.0\upsilon$ — No-one:** *"Do nothing."* Confusion Zone, stasis, physical/geological constraints without moral agency.
* **$-0.3\upsilon \text{ to } -0.5\upsilon$ — Only My Group:** *"Give to me."* In-group preference, tribal/national solidarity, clan protection, border fortresses.
* **$-1.0\upsilon$ — Only Me:** *"Give to me."* Egotism, self-interest, competitive accumulation at the expense of peers.
* **$-2.0\upsilon$ — No-One Else:** *"Take to give to me."* Pure parasitic extraction, total deception, active erasure of the other.

> **The Invariant Law of the Group Scale:**  
> If an ideal's structural definition is bounded to the in-group (the clan, the union, the nation-state, the race) and relies upon excluding or opposing an external outgroup, its beneficiary scope mathematically satisfies $\text{Scope} < 1.0$. **It sits on the negative side of the axis ($-0.3\upsilon$ to $-0.5\upsilon$), NOT in the positive universal quadrant.**

### 1.2 The Will Axis ($\psi$): Volitional Vector
The vertical axis measures the direction and quality of energetic intent:
* **$+2.0\psi$ — Productive Justice:** Systemic, compounding generation of value.
* **$+1.0\psi$ — Proactive Will ("Do"):** Building, asserting, creating, inventing, rebelling against tyranny.
* **$0.0\psi$ — Neutrality / Stasis:** Observation, equilibrium, passive waiting.
* **$-1.0\psi$ — Suppressive / Passive Will ("Do Not"):** Withholding, allowing, cutting down, stoic endurance, complacent avoidance.
* **$-2.0\psi$ — Destructive Force:** Active dismantling, entropy, chaos.

## 2. Potential Over Time: "The Way" vs. "Paths to Nowhere"

In Vector Field Theory, an idea's coordinate is its **initial condition** in a potential field. What determines its ultimate moral validity is whether its potential **increases or decreases over time**:

### 2.1 The Scale of Truth and Progress ("The Way" / Action $\eta$)
* Trajectory passing from $(0, 0)$ through $(+1\upsilon, +1\psi)$ toward **The Bliss Zone $(+2\upsilon, +2\psi)$**.
* **Criterion:** Potential over time **INCREASES**.
* An ideal belongs here if its core mechanism dissolves vertical hierarchy, reduces transaction costs between humans, and naturally expands from the individual to *Other Beings* and ultimately to *{Everyone}*.
* **It is the ONLY diagonal vector on the Hegemony that breaks out of the inner box.**

### 2.2 The Paths to Nowhere (Decreasing Potential)
* **The Scale of Lies and Regression ($\mathbf{TL \to TR}$):** High proactive will $(+\psi)$ harnessed to *Only My Group* or *Only Me* $(-υ)$. Even if an idea starts with high energy, as self/clan interest dominates, it descends into the **Greed Zone** and causes systemic friction/conflict. Potential collapses.
* **The Scale of Stagnation and Rot ($\mathbf{BL \to BR}$):** Passive will $(-ψ)$ paired with bounded or cynical avoidance. Leads into the **Bored, Sadness, Depression, and Destructive Zones**. Potential decays into structural collapse.

* **The Paradox:** If it *excludes non-mates*, it is bounded to *Only My Group* ($-0.4\upsilon$). Scoring it $+0.7\upsilon$ while claiming it excludes people was a glaring logical contradiction.
* **The Resolution (The Stranger Protocol):** In Australian social physics, Mateship is **not** a closed mafia or clan initiation. It is an **open-access, zero-cost protocol**. An Australian greets a total stranger on the street or in the outback as *"mate"* by default. Trust is granted *a priori* without asking for pedigree, religion, or background. You are only ejected if you actively betray the compact (scabbing, pulling rank, exploitation).
* **Derivation:**
  - Beneficiary Scope: Extends horizontally to any stranger $\to$ **Other Beings ($+0.8\upsilon$)**.
  - Will Vector: Active mutual binding, stopping for the stranded driver $\to$ **Proactive Will ($+0.5\psi$)**.
  - Trajectory: Compounds social trust, dissolves caste $\to$ **The Way ($\nearrow$)**.
* **Final Coordinate:** **$(+0.8, +0.5)$ — Greater Good**.

## 6. How Anyone Can Verify This Work

Any future auditor or researcher can independently verify the coordinates using this three-question protocol:

1. **Beneficiary Test:** Who does this ideal benefit when fully manifested?
   - Does it benefit all beings unconditionally without an excluded outgroup? $\to \mathbf{+1.0\upsilon \text{ to } +2.0\upsilon}$ ({Everyone} / Other Beings).
   - Does it benefit only "our people", the nation-state, the clan, or citizens behind a border? $\to \mathbf{-0.3\upsilon \text{ to } -0.5\upsilon}$ (Only My Group).
   - Does it hoard wealth or status for the self/insider? $\to \mathbf{-1.0\upsilon \text{ to } -2.0\upsilon}$ (Only Me / No-One Else).
2. **Volitional Test:** What is the energy doing?
   - Actively building, creating, or rebelling against oppression? $\to \mathbf{+1.0\psi \text{ to } +2.0\psi}$ (Proactive / Productive).
   - Passively enduring, neglecting, cutting down, or allowing rot? $\to \mathbf{-1.0\psi \text{ to } -2.0\psi}$ (Suppressive / Destructive).
3. **Trajectory Test:** What happens to its potential over time?
   - Does it move along **The Way** toward universal equity and the $(2,2)$ Bliss Zone? $\to$ **Scale of Truth and Progress**.
   - Does it get trapped in conflict, resource depletion, cynicism, or complacency? $\to$ **Paths to Nowhere**.
