## 3. Mathematical Mechanics (Operative Projections)

### Proven and Unproven Matrices
A worldview is represented mathematically by the interaction of two independent matrices of knowledge:
1. **Proven Knowledge ($P$)**: The set of verified experiential definitions where unknowns have been collapsed to a ground state of minimum entropy.
2. **Unproven Knowledge ($U$)**: The field of unproven assumptions, possibilities, and faith.

The total worldview is calculated as the absolute difference between these two matrices:
$$\text{Worldview} = \|P - U\| + \|U - P\|$$

Rather than forming a harmonious circle, plotting this interaction on a 3D coordinate graph yields a **hyperbolic paraboloid**—a saddle shape. This geometry demonstrates that a complete, balanced worldview must be built from two equal, opposing halves.

### The Equation of Judgment
The dynamic behavior of a thought within the mental field is determined by its **Will Vector ($W$)**, which measures the energy and complexity of a concept:
$$\text{Will Vector} = \text{Complexity} + \text{Resistance} - \text{Entropy}$$

An idea's final resistance value ($R_{final}$) determines its structural stability:
* **Dissolution ($R_{final} < 1.0$)**: The concept lacks sufficient ordering force and dissolves into entropic noise.
* **Truth ($R_{final} = 1.0$)**: The concept achieves perfect stability, indicating a frame-invariant truth that operates at zero entropy (Grace).
* **Tyranny ($R_{final} > 1.0$)**: The concept distorts toward infinity, forcing rigid containment structures onto the mind.

### The Path to Equilibrium
A system naturally settles into its most stable, probable state consistent with logical constraints through the principle of **Maximum Entropy (MaxEnt)**. When a mind achieves perfect self-awareness—defined as zero difference between actual knowledge and perceived knowledge (the coordinate of $Maybes = 0$)—the worldview achieves perfect geometric balance, allowing frictionless cognitive flow ($\gamma = 1.0$).
