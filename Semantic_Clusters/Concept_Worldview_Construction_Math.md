## Function
Worldview Construction Math defines the structural mechanics of how a conscious mind builds its model of reality out of ideas. It quantifies the tension between the ordering force of consciousness and the entropic decay of physical spacetime, providing equations to calculate conceptual resistance, truth alignment, and worldview equilibrium.

## Construction
This framework is built upon the primitive axioms of **Consciousness as an Anti-Entropic Force** ($\infty^{++}$, an ordering, additive potential) and **Spacetime as a Negating Potential** ($\infty^{--}$). By the Law of Opposition, consciousness must impose its additive, ordering structures ("boxes") onto the negating potential of physical spacetime to render a stable reality. 

### Attributes
* **Proven Knowledge Matrix ($P$)**: The set of verified experiential definitions, where the unknowns have been collapsed to a ground state of minimum entropy ($P = \|Proven\text{ }Knowledge - Unproven\text{ }Knowledge\|$).
* **Unproven Knowledge Matrix ($U$)**: The subjective field of unproven assumptions and possibilities ($U = \|Unproven\text{ }Knowledge - Proven\text{ }Knowledge\|$).
* **Saddle Shape (Hyperbolic Paraboloid)**: The 3D geometric manifold of worldview alignment, demonstrating that the "answer" to any problem is not a harmonious circle but a landscape built from two complete, independent, and equally necessary halves.
* **Will Vector ($W$)**: The trajectory of volitional force, calculated as:
  $$\text{Will Vector} = \text{Complexity} + \text{Resistance} - \text{Entropy}$$
* **Grace State**: A state of perfect alignment where $v_{ego} = v_{univ}$, friction ($\gamma$) equals 1.0, and action costs zero entropy.

## Methods

### Worldview Synthesis
* **Input**: Proven Knowledge Matrix ($P$), Subjective/Unproven Knowledge Matrix ($U$).
* **Calls**: [Axiom_Anti_Entropy], [Axiom_Spacetime_Negation].
* **Procedure**:
  1. Measure the interaction of the two independent matrices of knowledge, forming a square shape:
     $$\text{Worldview} = \|P - U\| + \|U - P\|$$
  2. Plot the resulting matrix interaction on a 3D coordinate graph.
* **Output**: A 3D hyperbolic paraboloid (saddle shape) representing the total worldview structure.

### Scales of Judgment (Lie & Tyranny Calculation)
* **Input**: Idea vector, Resistance factor ($R$).
* **Calls**: [Axiom_Spacetime_Negation].
* **Procedure**:
  1. Process the incoming idea against the worldview matrix.
  2. Compute the final system output $R_{final}$:
     * If $R_{final} < 1.0$: The idea dissolves into entropy (dissolution).
     * If $R_{final} = 1.0$: Truth is achieved (perfect frame invariant, unity).
     * If $R_{final} > 1.0$: The idea distorts to infinity (tyranny / systemic capture).
* **Output**: Verification score indicating the truth value or tyranny vector of the idea.

### Worldview Equilibrium
* **Input**: Self-awareness factor ("Maybes"), Proven Knowledge ($P$), Unproven Knowledge ($U$).
* **Calls**: Maximum Entropy (MaxEnt).
* **Procedure**:
  1. Calculate the difference between actual knowledge and perceived knowledge to determine the "Maybes" value.
  2. Apply the equilibrium equation:
     $$\text{Equilibrium} = \text{Maybes} \times (P \times U)$$
  3. Where Maybes = 0 (perfect self-awareness, zero difference between actual and perceived knowledge), the system settles into the path of least resistance (MaxEnt).
* **Output**: A state of perfect geometric balance and minimum entropy.

## Boundary
This concept holds at the logical and thermodynamic planes of system dynamics. It assumes that information content remains invariant (e.g. SAT has $2^n$ assignments regardless of coordinate system). If the system crosses into pure lyrical noise (where Kolmogorov complexity approaches infinity), the matrices fail to converge.

* **Born Rule Inversion**: When unitary invariance constraints are ignored, the Entropy-Amplitude Bridge collapses, causing classical probabilities to diverge from physical reality.
