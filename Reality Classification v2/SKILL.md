# Reality Classification v2 (AEC-First Architecture)

## 1. Core Inversion: Invariant-First, Not Word-First
In v2, words are **not** the primary keys:
* Primary keys are **AEC Invariant Triad IDs** (`action: [Action_1, Action_2, Action_3]`).
* Words are **surface descriptors / aliases** mapped to these invariant forms across contexts.
* Multiple words cluster into the same AEC primitive (e.g. *store, cache, reservoir, hoard* are all surface expressions of the same containment-retention lifecycle).

---

## 2. The 3-Step Action-Strain-Effect Pairwise Lockstep
Every AEC node is governed by the 1-to-1 pairwise progression:
```
Action_1 ──[Strain_1]──► Effect_1
Action_2 ──[Strain_2]──► Effect_2
Action_3 ──[Strain_3]──► Effect_3
```
* **Action (Current)**: Three sequential, atomic process verbs.
* **Strain (Distance)**: The three exact resisting forces or pulls overcome.
* **Effect (Ideal)**: The three resolved state signatures.

---

## 3. The 5-Stage Epistemic Primitive Hunter Sieve
All words and concepts must pass through the `epistemic-primitive-hunter` pipeline before being assigned to an AEC node:
1. **Substrate Evacuation**: Purge carbon biology (mouths, eyes, organs), Earth-bound Newtonian physics (gravity, atmospheric friction, arbitrary down without baseline), and IT jargon (buffers, telemetry).
2. **Q2 Potential Space Mapping (What)**: Identify the latent distinctions, degrees of freedom, and uncommitted gradients before action occurs.
3. **Q2 ➔ Q3 Collapse (Where)**: Identify the exact threshold crossing, force application (F · Δt), or boundary modification that localizes potential into bounded manifestation.
4. **3-Step Triad Extraction**: Frame the irreducible process in lean, atomic English.
5. **7-Plane Isomorphism Test**: Verify that the invariant projects cleanly across all 7 reality dimensions (Q1..Q7) without metaphor.

---

## 4. The Plane-Collapse Ban
The root `definitive_meaning`, `action`, `strain`, and `effect` of an AEC primitive must remain **strictly plane-agnostic**:
* **Banned in root definitions**: *physical, mental, emotional, biological, historical, social, logical, brain, body, computer*.
* Plane-specific manifestations belong exclusively inside the `planes` projection object (Q1..Q7).

---

## 5. Topological Closure Levels (Zero Glyphs)
Classify each AEC node by its pure topological boundary constraints:
* **Level 0 (Open Axis)**: Unconstrained traversal along a dimension (`move`, `go`, `change`).
* **Level 1 (Wall / Threshold)**: 1 direction blocked / barrier (`door`, `gate`, `enter`, `leave`, `shield`).
* **Level 2 (Corner / Intersection)**: 2 directions interacting (`cause`, `effect`, `collide`, `meet`).
* **Level 3 (Pocket / Channel)**: 3 directions blocked, 1 side open (`hold` active grip, `cradle`, `catch`).
* **Level 4 (Enclosure)**: All directions closed (`have` passive custody, `contain`).
* **Chamber / Volume**: Closed enclosure with internal capacity across time (`store`, `shelter`, `vault`).

---

## 6. Strict Plane Separation (Telos in Q4 Only)
* Purpose clauses (*"so that..."*, *"in order to..."*, *"to prevent..."*) belong **strictly in Plane Q4 (Lyrical / Meaning)**.
* Planes Q1, Q2, Q3, Q5, Q6, and Q7 must never contain purposive justifications.

---

## 7. The Evolutionary Convergence Loop: Grow, Simplify, Delete, Repeat
The library evolves through a continuous 4-phase cycle:
1. **Grow**: Inspect unmapped candidate words from foundational corpora (WordNet, Swadesh). Mint novel irreducible AEC forms when an unrepresented physical-informational force vector or boundary topology is discovered. Attach natural language words as primary labels, surface aliases, and planar manifestations.
2. **Simplify**: Polish all action, strain, and effect triads. Ensure each step is an atomic process verb in strict 1-to-1 lockstep. Purge all substrate artifacts.
3. **Delete / Prune**: Detect duplicate or overlapping action signatures. Merge near-clones into single canonical AEC Forms. Identify composite phenotypes and refactor them into children of their parent primitives. Repoint all mapped surface words to the canonical node.
4. **Repeat**: Re-number all Form IDs cleanly (`AEC-001`, `AEC-002`...), synchronize `aec_dictionary.json` and `aec_dictionary.js`, and run `validate_v2.py` (100% compliance mandatory).
