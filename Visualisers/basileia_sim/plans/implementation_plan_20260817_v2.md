# Basileia of Understanding - Redesign & Scale Calibration Plan (v2)

## User Feedback & Requirements
1. **Scale & Movement**: The previous walls were cramped, causing collision traps and suffocating movement. Scale must be widened significantly with generous navigable courtyards and open avenues matching the illustration.
2. **Knowledge System**: Remove artificial click-to-absorb shrine modals. Knowledge is purely driven by **traversal towards the apex corners**.
3. **Multi-Spectral Fog & Line of Sight**: Fog and LoS piercing are specific to each knowledge type. Moving deeper into a quadrant towards its apex generates that specific realm's understanding, piercing its corresponding obstacles and revealing its pathways.
4. **Visual Alignment with the Illustration**:
   - Central Walled City: Cathedral dome, green garden courtyards, fortified ramparts, wide gates.
   - Top-Left: Winding rivers, stepped hill ruins, apex forest sanctuary.
   - Top-Right: Stepped crimson ziggurat, radial concentric heat rings, apex tower, furnace gate.
   - Bottom-Right: Massive cracked caldera, central smoking column colosseum, fissure network, obsidian obelisks.
   - Bottom-Left: Grassland streams, central circular foundation, circular palisade camp with tents and campfire.

## Collision & Movement Mechanics
- Generous passages (minimum 80px wide).
- Continuous vector slide collision (prevent sticky corners).
- Dynamic line-of-sight raycasting that smoothly punches through walls as domain knowledge rises.
